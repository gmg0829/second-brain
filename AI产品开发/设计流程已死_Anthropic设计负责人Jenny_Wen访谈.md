## Executive Summary
## 执行摘要

Jenny Wen, Head of Design at Anthropic, discusses how AI is fundamentally transforming the role of designers. The traditional design process—mocking and prototyping comprising 60-70% of work—is giving way to a new paradigm where designers spend more time "jamming" with AI agents and consulting on AI outputs. The conversation explores Anthropic's unique design culture, the Skills Framework that emerged from an internal prototype called Cloud Studio, and three hiring archetypes for the AI era: Strong Generalist, Deep Specialist, and Correct New Grad.

Jenny Wen，Anthropic 设计负责人，在本期节目中探讨了 AI 如何从根本上改变设计师的角色。传统的"模拟+原型"设计流程曾占据设计师 60-70% 的工作时间，如今这一比例已降至 30-40%。设计师们开始将更多时间投入到与 AI 智能体的"即兴协作"(jamming) 和"咨询"(consulting) 中。访谈深入探讨了 Anthropic 独特的设计文化、从 Cloud Studio 原型演变为 Skills Framework 的历程，以及 AI 时代三种招聘原型：Strong Generalist（全面通才）、Deep Specialist（深度专才）和 Correct New Grad（合适应届生）。

## Core Thesis: The Death of Traditional Design Process
## 核心论点：传统设计流程的消亡

### The Old Paradigm
### 旧范式

Traditionally, designers spent 60-70% of their time on mocking and prototyping. This involved creating multiple visual iterations, sharing them with stakeholders, gathering feedback, and revising. The process was inherently sequential and time-consuming.

传统意义上，设计师将 60-70% 的时间花在模拟和原型制作上。这一过程包括创建多个视觉迭代版本、与利益相关者分享、收集反馈、反复修改。流程本质上是顺序的、耗时的。

### The New Reality
### 新现实

With AI tools like Claude Code and Cowork, the mocking/prototyping phase has shrunk to 30-40% of a designer's work. The rest involves:

随着 Claude Code 和 Cowork 等 AI 工具的出现，模拟/原型阶段已缩减至设计师工作的 30-40%。其余时间涉及：

- **Jamming**: Real-time collaboration with AI agents, course-correcting as you go
- **即兴协作 (Jamming)**：与 AI 智能体实时协作，边做边调整方向
- **Consulting**: Reviewing AI-generated outputs and providing guidance
- **咨询 (Consulting)**：审核 AI 生成的结果并提供指导
- **Rapid iteration**: AI can generate and iterate much faster than traditional mockups
- **快速迭代**：AI 生成和迭代的速度远快于传统原型制作

This shift is not about designers becoming engineers, but about evolving the design process to leverage AI's capabilities while maintaining human judgment and taste.

这种转变并非让设计师变成工程师，而是让设计流程进化以发挥 AI 的能力，同时保持人类的判断力和品味。

## Claude Cowork: A 10-Day Build Story
## Claude Cowork：10 天构建的故事

### The Origin
### 起源

The story of Claude Cowork began not with a formal design brief, but through organic discovery. A researcher showed Jenny a prototype called "Cloud Studio"—a dense, powerful interface built on top of an agentic harness that displayed Claude's knowledge, skills, and previewed outputs.

Claude Cowork 的故事并非始于正式的设计简报，而是通过有机发现。一位研究人员向 Jenny 展示了一个名为"Cloud Studio"的原型——一个建立在 AI 智能体框架之上的密集而强大的界面，可以展示 Claude 的知识、技能并预览输出。

### Initial Skepticism
### 最初的怀疑

Jenny admits she looked at Cloud Studio and thought, "I don't know what's going on. I don't really get it." But she noticed something important: there was significant internal energy around it, even if its value wasn't immediately clear.

Jenny 坦承，她看到 Cloud Studio 时想的是："我不知道这是什么。我真的不明白。"但她注意到一个重要现象：围绕它有大量内部热情，即使价值尚不明确。

### The Insight
### 洞察

This became a case study in what Jenny calls "ill legible ideas"—concepts that aren't yet understandable but show promise. The eventual output from Cloud Studio was the **Skills Framework**—markdown files that instruct Claude on how to do specific tasks.

这成为"ill legible ideas"（难以理解的想法）的案例研究——那些尚未被理解但显示出潜力的概念。Cloud Studio 的最终产物是 **Skills Framework**（技能框架）——一系列指导 Claude 如何执行特定任务的 Markdown 文件。

### Lesson Learned
### 经验教训

When working on Claude Cowork's form factor, Jenny drew inspiration from that early prototype, extracting elements like seeing Claude's plan, to-dos, and context into the final product. She suggests designers can be like VCs for internal prototypes—paying attention to interesting ideas even before they're fully articulable.

在为 Claude Cowork 构思形态时，Jenny 从早期原型中汲取灵感，将"查看 Claude 的计划、待办事项和上下文"等元素提取到最终产品中。她建议设计师可以像内部原型的 VC 一样——关注那些尚未完全成型但显示出活力的有趣想法。

## Three Hiring Archetypes
## 三种招聘原型

Jenny identified three distinct designer profiles that thrive in Anthropic's environment:

Jenny 确定了在 Anthropic 环境中蓬勃发展的三种截然不同的设计师类型：

### 1. Strong Generalist
### 1. 全面通才 (Strong Generalist)

These designers are highly versatile, capable of working across many different types of problems. They thrive in ambiguity and can pivot quickly between tasks. They embody the traditional "T-shaped" designer but with exceptional breadth.

这些设计师非常多才多艺，能够处理多种不同类型的问题。他们在模糊中茁壮成长，能够快速在不同任务间切换。他们体现了传统的"T 型"设计师，但具有卓越的广度。

### 2. Deep Specialist
### 2. 深度专才 (Deep Specialist)

These designers have exceptional depth in one particular area—may it be research, visual design, or a specific domain. Their expertise runs deep and they're the "go-to" person for specialized problems.

这些设计师在一个特定领域有非凡的深度——无论是研究、视觉设计还是特定领域。他们的专业知识深厚，是专门问题的"首选"人选。

### 3. Correct New Grad
### 3. 合适应届生 (Correct New Grad)

Counterintuitively, Anthropic sometimes hires new graduates who are "correct" for their culture, even without extensive experience. They look for people who demonstrate good judgment, curiosity, and alignment with Anthropic's values rather than just credentialing.

反直觉的是，Anthropic 有时会招聘没有丰富经验但"适合"其文化的应届生。他们寻找的是表现出良好判断力、好奇心和与 Anthropic 价值观一致的人，而不仅仅是 credentials。

### Key Insight
### 关键洞察

The common thread across all three archetypes is not specific skills or experiences, but rather:

三种原型的共同点不是特定的技能或经验，而是：

- Genuine excitement about the technology
- 对技术的真正热情
- History of building things
- 有构建事物的历史
- Desire to be on the frontier of what's possible
- 希望处于可能性的前沿

## The Legibility Framework
## Legibility Framework（可解读性框架）

One of Jenny's key contributions at Anthropic is the Legibility Framework. The core insight is that **context is critical for both humans and AI**.

Jenny 在 Anthropic 的关键贡献之一是 Legibility Framework。其核心洞察是：**上下文对人类和 AI 都至关重要**。

### The Problem
### 问题

When Claude generates code or content, it needs to understand the context in which it's operating—why certain decisions were made, what the goals are, and what constraints exist. Without this legibility, outputs can feel generic or misaligned.

当 Claude 生成代码或内容时，它需要理解其运行上下文——为什么做出某些决定、目标是什么、存在什么约束。没有这种可解读性，输出可能感觉通用或不对齐。

### The Framework
### 框架

Designers at Anthropic work on making AI systems more legible—ensuring that:

Anthropic 的设计师致力于使 AI 系统更具可解读性——确保：

- AI can reason about its own decision-making
- AI 能够推理自己的决策
- Humans can understand what the AI is doing and why
- 人类能够理解 AI 在做什么以及为什么
- Context is preserved and communicated effectively
- 上下文被有效保留和传达

This represents a new kind of design work: not designing screens, but designing the information architecture and communication patterns between humans and AI.

这代表了一种新的设计工作：不是设计屏幕，而是设计人类与 AI 之间的信息架构和通信模式。

## The Changing Role of Designers
## 设计师角色的演变

### From Mockups to Manifestos
### 从模拟到宣言

Rather than producing visual mockups for approval, designers increasingly produce "manifestos"—documents that articulate the vision, principles, and context that guide AI behavior. These replace the traditional "here's what we should build" mockups with "here's what we believe and why."

设计师不再制作供审批的视觉模拟稿，而是越来越多地制作"宣言"(manifestos)——阐述愿景、原则和指导 AI 行为的上下文。这些取代了传统的"我们应该构建什么"模拟稿，变成"我们相信什么以及为什么"。

### Continuous Collaboration
### 持续协作

The design process is no longer a handoff but a continuous conversation. Designers:

设计流程不再是交接，而是持续的对话。设计师：

- Work alongside engineers daily, not in sequential phases
- 每天与工程师并肩工作，而非顺序阶段
- Review AI outputs in real-time and course-correct
- 实时审核 AI 输出并调整方向
- Maintain ongoing relationships with the products they design
- 与他们设计的产品保持持续的关系

### The "Best Teams Roast Each Other" Culture
### "最好的团队会互相批评"的文化

Anthropic fosters a culture where teams directly and constructively criticize each other's work. This isn't about being harsh—it's about being honest and direct to get to the best outcomes. Jenny credits this culture with enabling the rapid, high-quality output the team produces.

Anthropic 培养了一种团队直接且建设性地批评彼此工作的文化。这不是关于刻薄——而是关于坦诚和直接以获得最佳结果。Jenny 将这种文化归功于团队能够快速产出高质量成果。

## Key Takeaways
## 关键要点

1. **The design process is not dying—it's evolving.** Mocking and prototyping are being replaced by jamming and consulting, but the core design skills of taste, judgment, and systems thinking remain essential.
   **设计流程没有消亡——它在进化。** 模拟和原型正在被即兴协作和咨询取代，但品味、判断力和系统思维等核心设计技能仍然至关重要。

2. **Designers should embrace "ill legible ideas."** Pay attention to prototypes and concepts that don't immediately make sense but show energy and potential.
   **设计师应该拥抱"难以理解的想法"。** 关注那些没有立即有意义但显示出潜力和活力的原型和概念。

3. **Three archetypes succeed at Anthropic**: Strong Generalists, Deep Specialists, and Correct New Grads—united by excitement about the technology and desire to build.
   **三种原型在 Anthropic 取得成功**：全面通才、深度专才和合适应届生——共同点是对技术的热情和构建的欲望。

4. **Context is the new craft.** Making AI systems legible—understandable to humans and itself—is a fundamental design challenge of our time.
   **上下文是新的工艺。** 使 AI 系统可解读——对人类和 AI 本身都可理解——是我们这个时代的基本设计挑战。

5. **The best teams are direct.** "Best teams roast each other" reflects a culture of honest, constructive feedback that produces better outcomes.
   **最好的团队是直接的。** "最好的团队互相批评"反映了一种坦诚、建设性反馈的文化，能产生更好的结果。

6. **Stay humble and resilient.** As the industry changes rapidly, the ability to adapt while maintaining core values is crucial.
   **保持谦逊和韧性。** 随着行业快速变化，在保持核心价值观的同时适应变化的能力至关重要。
