# Head of Growth (Anthropic): Anthropic is Automating Its Own Growth

## Overview | 概述

Amol Avasare is Head of Growth at Anthropic, which has achieved the most unprecedented growth trajectory in history—scaling from $1 billion to over $19 billion in ARR in just 14 months. Previously, Amol worked on growth teams at Mercury and MasterClass. Before that he was a founder, and he cold emailed his way into the Anthropic role when no job listing existed. Most remarkably, he overcame a traumatic brain injury from a Muay Thai match that meant he couldn't work for nearly a year.

---

## 1. The Story of Cold Emailing Mike Krieger | 给 Mike Krieger 发冷邮件的故事

**English:**

Amol's journey to Anthropic is a masterclass in creative networking. When no growth role was listed, he cold emailed Anthropic's CPO Mike Krieger (co-founder of Instagram) directly. The email wasn't about asking for a job—it was about demonstrating his thinking on growth. He articulated a clear perspective on how Anthropic should think about growth, which led to a conversation and eventually a role. This exemplifies his philosophy: **don't wait for opportunities, create them**.

**中文:**

Amol 加入 Anthropic 的经历是创意社交的经典案例。当没有列出增长岗位时，他直接给 Anthropic 的 CPO Mike Krieger（Instagram 联合创始人）发了冷邮件。这封邮件不是关于求职——而是展示他对增长的理解。他清晰地阐述了对 Anthropic 应该如何思考增长的看法，这引发了一次对话，最终获得了一个职位。这体现了他的人生哲学：**不要等待机会，要创造机会**。

## 2. Leading Growth at the Fastest-Growing Company Ever | 在史上增长最快的公司领导增长

**English:**

Anthropic's growth is unprecedented. In 14 months, they went from $1B to $19B ARR. But Amol is candid about the challenges: "Success disasters" happen when you grow so fast that infrastructure can't keep up. The biggest challenge isn't finding growth levers—it's organizational bandwidth. Teams are stretched thin, and the speed of progress means there's never enough time to think deeply. The culture is extremely scrappy: minimize bureaucracy, just go.

**中文:**

Anthropic 的增长是史无前例的。14 个月内，他们从 10 亿美元 ARR 增长到 190 亿美元。但 Amol 坦诚地谈到挑战："成功的灾难"发生得如此之快，基础设施跟不上。最大的挑战不是找到增长杠杆——而是组织带宽。团队捉襟见肘，进度的速度意味着永远没有足够的时间深入思考。文化是非常务实的：最小化官僚主义，直接行动。

## 3. What the Growth Team Actually Does | 增长团队实际做什么

**English:**

The Anthropic growth team is roughly 40 people, structured in two ways:
- **Horizontal functions**: Growth Platform, Monetization—thinking about growth across all products
- **Vertical audience pods**: B2B Growth, Claude Code Growth, Knowledge Worker Growth, API Growth—keeping focus narrow when you have so many different products

The team includes engineers, designers, PMs, and data scientists. But here's what makes them different: **they index 70/30 toward big bets rather than micro-optimizations**. Most growth teams would spend 60-70% of time on small optimizations. Anthropic flips this—most resources go to big swings that can move the needle dramatically.

**中文:**

Anthropic 增长团队大约 40 人，分为两种结构：
- **横向职能**：增长平台、变现——思考所有产品的增长
- **纵向受众团队**：B2B 增长、Claude Code 增长、知识工作者增长、API 增长——当你有这么多不同产品时保持专注

团队包括工程师、设计师、PM 和数据科学家。但让他们不同的是：**他们按 70/30 的比例偏向大赌注而不是小优化**。大多数增长团队会将 60-70% 的时间花在小的优化上。Anthropic 翻转了这个——大部分资源用于可以大幅移动指针的大动作。

## 4. Why Activation is the Biggest Challenge in AI Products | 为什么激活是 AI 产品中最大的挑战

**English:**

Amol identifies activation as the single highest-leverage growth problem in AI. The challenge is that AI capabilities are evolving so fast that by the time you ship a new flow, a new model may have already made those learnings irrelevant. The model capabilities are rising so quickly that:

1. You ship a new flow based on current model capabilities
2. By the time you get learnings, a new model unlocks newer capabilities
3. All those learnings become obsolete

The solution is finding the right product/feature for the right user—and being willing to add friction to do that correctly. **The right friction helps**, and adding friction usually works if you do it the right way. It's about identifying the characteristics of a user that allows you to recommend them the right feature or product.

**中文:**

Amol 将激活确定为 AI 领域中杠杆效应最高的增长问题。挑战在于 AI 能力发展得非常快，以至于当你发布一个新的流程时，新模型可能已经让这些学习变得过时了。模型能力提升如此之快：

1. 你根据当前模型能力发布新流程
2. 当你获得学习时，新模型解锁了更新的能力
3. 所有这些学习都变得过时

解决方案是为正确的用户找到正确的产品/功能——并且愿意添加摩擦来正确地做到这一点。**正确的摩擦是有帮助的**，如果你以正确的方式做，添加摩擦通常有效。这关乎识别用户的特征，使你能够向他们推荐正确的功能或产品。

## 5. Improving Onboarding: Lessons from Mercury | 改进入职：来自 Mercury 的教训

**English:**

At Mercury, Amol learned the power of intentional onboarding. The key insight: **people don't naturally know the best way to use AI products**. If people's instinct is to come to the product and ask basic questions like "what's the weather in SF?", they're not getting the most out of it. The job of growth isn't just acquisition—it's making sure users understand what the AI tools are capable of, to help them decide to use it and stick with it.

The biggest onboarding wins come from showing users the magic of the product quickly—demonstrating actual value rather than letting users stumble through basic interactions.

**中文:**

在 Mercury，Amol 学到了有意设计入职流程的力量。关键洞察：**人们不会本能地知道使用 AI 产品的最佳方式**。如果用户的本能是来到产品前问基本问题，比如"旧金山的天气怎么样？"，他们就没有充分利用产品。增长的工作不仅仅是获客——还要确保用户了解 AI 工具的能力，帮助他们决定使用并坚持使用它。

最大的入职胜利来自快速向用户展示产品的魔力——展示实际价值，而不是让用户通过基本交互跌跌撞撞。

## 6. The 70/30 Big Bets Strategy | 70/30 大赌注策略

**English:**

Unlike typical growth teams that focus on micro-optimizations, Anthropic indexes heavily toward big bets. The philosophy: if you have 20+ engineers, is the highest leverage of your time to ship an extra feature, or to figure out how to uplevel everything you're doing?

There's also an element of "shipping to learn"—building a prototype so you can have a better opinion. Many people talk about just trying three things and seeing how it goes. But at Anthropic, they take this further: **prototyping and showing it is better than talking about it**. A demo is worth a thousand discussions.

About 60-70% of what they ship doesn't have a PRD. Amol is averse to PRDs—"I just hate documentation. Just go, just cut the blockers." The remaining 20-30% is for things that are really important to get right, where documentation should be really good.

**中文:**

与典型的专注于微优化的增长团队不同，Anthropic 严重偏向大赌注。哲学是：如果你有 20 多名工程师，最高杠杆是发布一个额外的功能，还是弄清楚如何提升你所做的一切？

还有一个"边做边学"的因素——构建一个原型，以便你能有更好的看法。许多人谈论只是尝试三件事看看结果如何。但在 Anthropic，他们更进一步：**原型和展示比谈论它更好**。一个演示抵得上一千次讨论。

大约 60-70% 的产品没有 PRD。Amol 讨厌 PRD——"我就是讨厌文档。直接行动，切断障碍物。"剩余的 20-30% 是用于那些确实很重要的事情，文档应该真的很好。

## 7. Automating Growth Experiments with Claude (CASH) | 用 Claude 自动化增长实验 (CASH)

**English:**

Anthropic has built internal tools to automate growth experimentation. The system automatically runs experiments, analyzes results, and generates insights. Amol describes it as "CASH"—Claude Analysis Something for Growth (his playful name).

Key capabilities:
- **Automated chart analysis**: Every morning, Claude analyzes 20-25 charts automatically, surfacing anomalies and interesting patterns
- **Experiment generation**: Claude can suggest what experiments to run based on data patterns
- **Inbox management**: Claude does first-pass clearing of email, archives, sorts
- **Expense management**: Claude files reimbursements through Brex and expenses through other systems

The system watches metrics, the market, the roadmap, what's working and not—and says "here's what I think we should do now, here's the pivot we should take." It's like a strategy bot that constantly watches everything and distills insights.

**中文:**

Anthropic 构建了内部工具来自动化增长实验。该系统自动运行实验、分析结果并生成洞察。Amol 称之为"CASH"——Claude Analysis Something for Growth（他起的玩笑名字）。

关键能力：
- **自动化图表分析**：每天早晨，Claude 自动分析 20-25 张图表，突出异常和有趣模式
- **实验生成**：Claude 可以根据数据模式建议运行哪些实验
- **收件箱管理**：Claude 进行电子邮件的第一轮清理、归档、分类
- **费用管理**：Claude 通过 Brex 提交报销，通过其他系统提交费用

系统监视指标、市场、路线图、什么有效什么无效——然后说"这是我现在认为我们应该做的，这是我们应该进行的转变"。它就像一个策略机器人，不断观察一切并提炼洞察。

## 8. The Future of PM, Engineering, and Design Roles | PM、工程和设计角色的未来

**English:**

Amol predicts a significant shift in team composition as AI makes engineers exponentially more productive. The ratio may need to flip—**you might need more PMs than engineers** in the future. When engineers can build 10x faster with AI tools, the bottleneck shifts from execution to strategy and prioritization.

This means:
- **Engineers become mini-PMs**: With AI handling much of the execution, engineers need more product thinking
- **PMs become more important**: Someone needs to decide what to build, and that's where PMs add value
- **Prototyping changes**: With AI, a PM can prototype and show an idea rather than writing a PRD and waiting for engineering

The implication is a shift toward interdisciplinary skills. Engineers who understand product and PMs who understand technical constraints will be most valuable.

**中文:**

Amol 预测随着 AI 使工程师的 productivity 指数级提升，团队组成将发生重大变化。比例可能需要翻转——**未来你可能需要更多的 PM 而不是工程师**。当工程师用 AI 工具可以快 10 倍构建时，瓶颈从执行转向战略和优先级。

这意味着：
- **工程师成为小型 PM**：随着 AI 处理大部分执行，工程师需要更多的产品思维
- **PM 变得更加重要**：需要有人决定构建什么，这就是 PM 增加价值的地方
- **原型设计改变**：有了 AI，PM 可以原型和展示一个想法，而不是写 PRD 等待工程

这意味着向跨学科技能的转变。理解产品的工程师和理解技术约束的 PM 将最有价值。

## 9. The Manager Lens: AI Coaching | 管理者视角：AI 辅导

**English:**

One of the most innovative uses of AI at Anthropic is the "manager lens"—an AI version of your manager that provides coaching. This is part of Claude Cowork's capabilities: analyzing Slack conversations, detecting misalignment, and providing feedback.

Amol describes it as: "The stuff that's quite interesting is the man the manager lens where you can have like a version of your manager, coach you, give you feedback on how you're doing, how your communication is being perceived."

This scales management and feedback in ways that weren't previously possible. New employees can get real-time feedback on their communication and collaboration, helping them improve faster than traditional onboarding would allow.

**中文:**

Anthropic 最创新的 AI 用法之一是"管理者视角"——一个 AI 版本的管理者，提供辅导。这是 Claude Cowork 功能的一部分：分析 Slack 对话、检测不对齐，并提供反馈。

Amol 描述为："有趣的是管理者视角，你可以有一个版本的管理者，辅导你，就你做得如何、你的沟通如何被感知提供反馈。"

这以前所未有的方式扩展了管理和反馈。员工可以获得关于沟通和协作的实时反馈，帮助他们比传统入职更快改进。

## 10. AI Safety as a Core Mission | 作为核心使命的 AI 安全

**English:**

Anthropic's focus on AI safety isn't just marketing—it's baked into their strategy. Early in their history, they had a version of Claude before ChatGPT launched, but they chose not to launch it for safety reasons. They didn't want to kick off an AI global arms race.

This focus created a flywheel: their investment in AI coding and safety research made their models better, which accelerated their growth. The discipline of thinking about long-term implications of AI actually improved their product strategy.

**中文:**

Anthropic 对 AI 安全的关注不仅仅是营销——它是融入战略的。在他们历史的早期，ChatGPT 发布前他们有一个版本的 Claude，但他们选择不出于安全原因发布。他们不想引发 AI 全球军备竞赛。

这种关注创造了一个飞轮：他们对 AI 编码和安全研究的投资使他们的模型更好，这加速了他们的增长。思考 AI 长期影响的 discipline 实际上改善了他们的产品策略。

## 11. The Notebook Channels on Slack | Slack 上的笔记本频道

**English:**

Anthropic has a unique culture practice called "notebook channels"—essentially an internal Twitter feed where people share what they're thinking about and what their priorities are. Dario shares his thinking here, and everyone has one.

The purpose is twofold:
1. **Keep people updated**: A way to share what's working, what's not
2. **Scale beliefs across the org**: As the company grows quickly, you need ways to scale your leadership and culture

It's about modeling behaviors and principles. When the CEO shares their thinking transparently, it sets the tone for the entire organization.

**中文:**

Anthropic 有一种独特的文化实践叫做"笔记本频道"——本质上是内部 Twitter feed，人们分享他们在想什么、他们的优先事项是什么。Dario 在这里分享他的想法，每个人都有一个。

目的是双重的：
1. **保持人们更新**：分享什么有效、什么无效的方式
2. **在组织中扩展信念**：随着公司快速发展，你需要方式扩展你的领导和文化

这是关于 modeling 行为和原则。当 CEO 透明地分享他的想法时，它为整个组织设定了基调。

## 12. Constraints as Freedom | 约束作为自由

**English:**

Amol's most personal story is about his traumatic brain injury from a Muay Thai match—a kick to the head that meant he couldn't work for nearly a year. The meditation and recovery process taught him about the power of constraints.

When you have constraints—personal or professional—you're forced to focus. You're forced to adapt. In a business setting, you have to focus more. The meditation teacher said: "True freedom in life is learning how to be content when you don't get what you want."

This philosophy influences his approach at Anthropic: with constraints comes focus, and with focus comes freedom. The culture of being scrappy, minimizing bureaucracy, just going—it's about leveraging constraints as a competitive advantage.

**中文:**

Amol 最个人化的故事是关于他因泰拳比赛（一记踢到头部）导致的创伤性脑损伤，这意味着他将近一年无法工作。冥想和康复过程教会了他约束的力量。

当你有约束——个人的或职业的——你被迫专注。你被迫适应。在商业环境中，你必须更专注。冥想老师说："生命中真正的自由是学会如何在得不到想要的东西时感到满足。"

这种哲学影响了他在 Anthropic 的方法：有了约束就有了专注，有了专注就有了自由。务实的文化、最小化官僚主义、直接行动——这是关于将约束作为竞争优势。

## 13. Advice for Thriving in an AI-First Future | 在 AI 优先的未来中蓬勃发展的建议

**English:**

Amol's advice for thriving in an AI-first world:
1. **Use the tools**: Be on top of Claude Code, Cowork, understanding each model release and what new things you can do with it
2. **Get product sense around AI products**: AI capabilities are evolving so fast that understanding how to apply them to your job is crucial
3. **Lean into your competitive advantage**: If you're really good at craft, double down on it. If you're great at stakeholder mediation, double down. Identify your spike and become the best at that thing
4. **Be interdisciplinary**: In a world where engineers are mini-PMs, the ability to work across functions is increasingly valuable

**中文:**

Amol 在 AI 优先的世界中蓬勃发展的建议：
1. **使用工具**：掌握 Claude Code、Cowork，理解每个模型发布以及你可以用它做什么新事情
2. **获得 AI 产品的产品感**：AI 能力发展得如此之快，理解如何将它们应用到你自己的工作至关重要
3. **发挥你的竞争优势**：如果你擅长工艺，深耕它。如果你擅长利益相关者调解，深耕它。识别你的长处并成为最好的那一个
4. **跨学科**：在工程师成为小型 PM 的世界里，跨职能工作的能力越来越有价值

## Summary | 总结

Amol Avasare's journey from a traumatic brain injury to leading growth at one of the most successful companies in history is itself a story about the power of constraints and focus. At Anthropic, he demonstrates how AI can be leveraged not just for product but for operational excellence—automating experiment analysis, providing manager-level coaching, and scaling cultural practices like notebook channels.

The key insights for growth practitioners:
1. **Activation remains the highest-leverage problem in AI**—and proper friction can help
2. **Big bets > micro-optimizations**—70/30 strategy focusing on swings that move the needle
3. **The ratio of PMs to engineers may need to flip** as AI makes engineers more productive
4. **Prototyping beats documentation**—show, don't tell
5. **Constraints create focus, focus creates freedom**

The story of Anthropic's growth from $1B to $19B in 14 months isn't just about market timing or product-market fit—it's about a culture that embraces scrappiness, focuses on big bets, and uses AI to automate not just product features but operational excellence.
