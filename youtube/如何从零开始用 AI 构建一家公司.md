
# YC on How to Build a Company with AI from the Ground Up

# YC：如何从零开始用 AI 构建一家公司

---

## AI as Operating System, Not Tool

## AI 是操作系统，而非工具

- AI should not be a tool your company just uses, should be the OS your company runs on
    
- AI 不应该只是公司使用的工具，而应该是公司运行所依托的操作系统
    
- Every workflow, every decision, and every process should flow through an intelligent layer
    
- 每一个工作流、每一个决策、每一个流程都应该流经一个智能层
    
- The right person with AI tools can now build features that used to require an entire team
    
- 合适的人借助 AI 工具，如今可以独自构建曾经需要整个团队才能完成的功能
    

---

## Closed Loops Everywhere

## 无处不在的闭环

- In the old world, companies ran as open loops. You made a decision, executed it, and didn't systematically measure the outcome
    
- 在过去，公司以开环方式运转。你做出决策、执行决策，却不系统性地衡量结果
    
- A closed loop is self-regulating. It continuously monitors its output and adjusts its process to better meet the stated goal
    
- 闭环是自我调节的。它持续监控输出结果，并不断调整流程以更好地达成既定目标
    
- Every important process should be captured by an intelligent closed loop that captures information, feeds it back, and improves over time
    
- 每一个重要流程都应该被一个智能闭环所捕获，该闭环负责收集信息、反馈信息，并随着时间推移持续改进
    

---

## Make Your Company Queryable

## 让你的公司可被查询

- The whole organization should be legible to AI
    
- 整个组织应该对 AI 透明可读
    
- Every important action should produce an artifact that the intelligence at the center of the company can learn from
    
- 每一个重要行动都应产生一个制品，供公司核心智能系统从中学习
    
- Record meetings with AI notetakers, minimize DMs and emails, embed agents in comms
    
- 用 AI 记录员记录会议，减少私信和邮件，在通讯工具中嵌入智能代理
    
- Build custom dashboards with everything: revenue, sales, engineering, hiring, ops
    
- 构建涵盖一切的自定义仪表盘：营收、销售、工程、招聘、运营
    
- Provide models with as much context as you would provide an employee
    
- 向模型提供与员工同等程度的上下文信息

## Software Factories

## 软件工厂

- The next evolution of test-driven development
    
- 这是测试驱动开发的下一次进化
    
- Humans write a spec and a set of tests that define success, then AI agents generate the implementation and iterate until the tests pass
    
- 人类编写规格说明和定义成功标准的测试集，AI 代理生成实现代码并不断迭代直到测试通过
    
- The human defines what to build and judges the output, actual code is the agent's job
    
- 人类定义构建目标并评判输出结果，实际编码是 AI 代理的工作
    
- Some companies already have repos that contain no handwritten code, just specs and test harnesses
    
- 部分公司的代码库中已经没有人工编写的代码，只有规格说明和测试框架

## No More Human Middleware

## 不再需要人工中间层

- The classic management hierarchy no longer makes sense
    
- 传统的管理层级制度已不再适用
    
- If your company is queryable, artifact-rich, and legible to AI, you should have almost no human middleware
    
- 如果你的公司可被查询、制品丰富且对 AI 透明，你应该几乎不需要人工中间层
    
- Your company's velocity is only as fast as its information flow. Every layer of human routing you remove is a direct speed gain
    
- 公司的速度取决于信息流动的速度。每减少一层人工信息传递，就是直接提速

## Three Employee Archetypes (per Jack Dorsey)

## 三种员工原型（源自 Jack Dorsey）

- **IC / builder-operator**: directly makes and runs things. Not limited to engineers. Everyone builds. Everyone comes to meetings with working prototypes, not pitch decks.
    
- **个人贡献者 / 构建者-运营者**：直接创造并运营事物。不局限于工程师。人人参与构建。每个人带着可运行的原型参加会议，而不是 PPT
    
- **DRI (directly responsible individual)**: focused on strategy and customer outcomes. Not a classic manager. One person, one outcome, no hiding.
    
- **直接责任人（DRI）**：专注于战略和客户成果。不是传统意义上的管理者。一人负责，一个结果，无处遁形
    
- **AI founder**: still builds, still coaches, leads by example. If you're the founder, this needs to be you at the forefront, not delegating your AI strategy to someone else.
    
- **AI 创始人**：仍然亲自构建，仍然辅导团队，以身作则。如果你是创始人，你必须冲在最前线，而不是将 AI 战略委托给别人

## Token-Max, Not Headcount-Max

## 最大化 Token 用量，而非人员规模

- Maximizing token usage, not headcount, will be the critical shift
    
- 最大化 Token 使用量而非人员规模，将是关键转变
    
- The best companies will be the ones that are token-maxing
    
- 最优秀的公司将是那些将 Token 用量最大化的公司
    
- Be willing to run an uncomfortably high API bill because it's replacing what would have taken a far more expensive and inflated headcount
    
- 愿意承受看起来高得让人不安的 API 账单，因为这替代的是更昂贵、更臃肿的人员成本
    
- Dramatically leaner engineering, design, HR, and admin teams
    
- 大幅精简工程、设计、人力资源和行政团队

## The Early-Stage Advantage

## 早期阶段的优势

- You don't have legacy systems, org charts, or thousands of people to retrain
    
- 你没有遗留系统、组织架构图，也不需要对数千名员工进行再培训
    
- Existing companies have to maintain a live product while unwinding years of standard operating procedures. Every change risks breaking something that already works
    
- 现有公司必须在维护现有产品运转的同时，逐步瓦解多年积累的标准操作流程。每一次变革都可能破坏现有运转正常的事物
    
- Startups can design their systems, workflows, and culture around AI from the start, and can operate a thousand times faster than the incumbents
    
- 初创公司可以从一开始就围绕 AI 设计系统、工作流和文化，运转速度可以比行业巨头快一千倍
    
- You cannot outsource your conviction on the power of these tools. You need to develop it yourself by actually sitting with coding agents and using them
    
- 你无法将对这些工具力量的信念外包给他人。你必须亲自坐下来使用编程代理，通过实践来建立这种信念
