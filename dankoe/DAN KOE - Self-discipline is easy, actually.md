# Self-discipline is easy, actually
# 自律其实很简单

> 来源 / Source: [Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/self-discipline-is-easy-actually/)
> 日期 / Date: 2026

---

## 核心观点 / Core Insight

If you have to force yourself to do it, you will lose.
如果你必须强迫自己去做，你会输。

That's what most people get wrong about discipline.
这就是大多数人对自律的错误理解。

They hear that word… discipline… and a montage of grueling workouts with David Goggins running and screaming starts playing in their mind.
他们听到"自律"这个词……脑海里就开始播放大卫·戈金斯跑步和尖叫的艰苦训练画面。

They think they need to lash themselves to do the work that leads to something good in their life.
他们以为需要鞭策自己去做那些能带来美好生活的工作。

---

## 痛苦与自律 / Pain and Discipline

"The pain of discipline must outweigh the pain of laziness" has a lot of truth to it, as does struggle being the part of a story worth telling, but most successful people didn't hate the entire journey.
"自律的痛苦必须超过懒惰的痛苦"这句话很有道理，奋斗也是值得讲述的故事的一部分，但大多数成功者并不讨厌整个旅程。

In fact, it was fun to them.
事实上，他们觉得很有趣。

They wanted to do the hard thing.
他们想要做困难的事情。

And that points to a massive insight:
这就指向了一个重要的洞见：

---

## 你已经很自律了 / You're Already Disciplined

You are already disciplined toward the exact goals you are supposed to be.
你已经在为你应该追求的目标自律了。

That's how the mind works.
这就是大脑的工作方式。

> Goals are conditioned into your mind by your environment
> 目标是由你的环境灌输到你大脑中的

> Your goals become your filter for reality, shaping what you focus on and what you forget
> 你的目标成为你现实的过滤器，决定你关注什么、忽略什么

> Your brain helps you notice and remember information that helps achieve those goals
> 你的大脑帮助你注意和记住有助于实现这些目标的信息

> Naturally, you act toward those goals and your identity solidifies
> 自然地，你朝着这些目标行动，你的身份认同得到巩固

---

## 自律是身份认同的特征 / Discipline is a Feature of Identity

Someone who deeply identifies as a good student (after an entire childhood of their parents telling them they should pursue that goal) doesn't have to force themselves to study for 12 hours a day.
一个深度认同自己是好学生的人（从小父母告诉他们应该追求这个目标）不需要强迫自己每天学习12小时。

It's just who they are.
这就是他们是谁。

A bodybuilder who went through a breakup and was tired of having low self-confidence doesn't struggle to eat healthy or show up to the gym every day.
一个经历了分手、厌倦了自卑的健身者，不会为每天健康饮食或去健身房而挣扎。

In fact, it is painful if they don't.
事实上，如果不做反而痛苦。

Because if they don't, they aren't any closer to their goal, and since that goal is so intertwined with who they are, they feel as if their survival is at stake, because it is.
因为如果不这样做，他们离目标就不会更近，而由于这个目标与他们的身份如此紧密相连，他们感觉自己的生存受到了威胁事实就是如此。

Humans survive on both the physical and conceptual level (genics and memetics), we feel threatened if something important to us is threatened, no matter if it's our pet dog or pet belief.
人类在物质和概念两个层面生存（基因和模因），如果对我们重要的东西受到威胁，我们就会感到受到威胁，无论是我们的宠物狗还是我们坚持的信念。

## 有趣的观察 / Interesting Observation

A gamer doesn't have to force themselves to stare at the computer and perform for multiple hours at a time.
一个游戏玩家不需要强迫自己盯着电脑好几个小时。

Someone who loves a TV show will lie down and binge-watch the entire series.
一个爱看电视剧的人会躺下来一口气看完整个剧集。

Someone who procrastinates their work – because that goal doesn't align with them – will endlessly scroll their phone because their goal is to not achieve their goal.
一个拖延工作的人——因为那个目标与他们不符——会无休止地刷手机，因为他们的目标是"不实现目标"。

These people aren't "lazy," they're arguably self-disciplined, just toward a goal that results in immediate reward.
这些人不是"懒惰"，可以说他们是自律的，只是朝向一个立即得到回报的目标。

That's what discipline is, no? Persistent effort toward a goal.
这就是自律，不是吗？坚持不懈地追求一个目标。

And boy is the average person persistent toward a mediocre life.
而且普通人对平庸生活的坚持真是惊人。

Yes, most people are simply unconscious of the fact that their desire for comfort outweighs their desire for change.
是的，大多数人只是没有意识到他们追求舒适的需求超过了追求改变的需求。

It is, in fact, their goal.
事实上，这就是他们的目标。

## 舒适区就是目标 / Comfort is the Goal

"But Dan! Those are easy and comfortable! They don't take effort to do!"
"但是丹！那些事情很容易很舒服！不需要努力！"

Sure, and writing every day is comfortable for me.
当然，对我来说每天写作也很舒服。

It's more painful for me not to go to the gym than it is to go to the gym.
对我来说，不去健身房比去健身房更痛苦。

I try to play video games like I did when I was younger and I just can't.
我试着像年轻时那样玩电子游戏，但就是做不到。

They're boring and overstimulating.
它们既无聊又过度刺激。

I have better things to do.
我有更好的事情要做。

## 人类行为是有目的的 / Human Behavior is Teleological

Human behavior is teleological. Cybernetic.
人类行为是有目的的。是控制论的。

The mind is a goal-achieving machine.
大脑是一台目标实现机器。

We interpret the world with our identity as the lens, and the goals conditioned into our minds shape the behavior that forges who we are.
我们以身份认同为 lens 来解释世界，灌输到我们头脑中的目标塑造了锻造我们是谁的行为。

If we can understand this process, or reverse engineer it, we can leverage it to achieve nearly impossible goals.
如果我们能理解这个过程，或者逆向工程它，我们可以利用它来实现几乎不可能的目标。

## 你在 limbo 中 / Limbo is the Laboratory

The reason you want to be disciplined is that you've exhausted your current stage of life.
你想要自律的原因是你已经过够了当前的生活阶段。

You're starting to notice that you've stalled out.
你开始注意到自己已经停滞不前。

You're starting to notice that every day is the same as the last.
你开始注意到每一天都和昨天一样。

You're starting to notice that if you keep doing the same things, you won't achieve the life you want.
你开始注意到如果继续做同样的事情，你无法实现你想要的生活。

You're in limbo, and you don't want to be there anymore.
你处于 limbo（ limbo：不确定的状态），你不想再待在那里。

It's painful.
这很痛苦。

But you misinterpret that pain and think of the first solution that comes to mind: be more disciplined.
但你误解了这种痛苦，想到了第一个想到的解决方案：更加自律。

This is where most people get stuck in a loop of forcing themselves to do something they don't want to do, just to quit two weeks later and go back to the life they swore against.
这就是大多数人陷入的循环：强迫自己做不想做的事情，两周后放弃，回到他们发誓要摆脱的生活。

## 痛苦是改变的信号 / Pain is the Signal

Pain is the signal that change is happening.
痛苦是改变正在发生的信号。

The way out is to lean in.
出路是直面它。

To become aware of how painful your current life is.
意识到你现在的生活有多痛苦。

Because once the pain of where you are outweighs the pain of where you want to be, discipline is no longer painful, because you don't have to force yourself to do it.
因为一旦你所在地方的痛苦超过了你想要去的地方的痛苦，自律就不再痛苦，因为你不需要强迫自己去做。

## 第一步：写下来 / Step One: Write It Out

Step one is to write out:
第一步是写下：

* Everything you hate about your current life (I don't want to hear about positive thinking right now, you're wrong).
* 你讨厌当前生活的所有事情（我现在不想听什么积极思考，你错了）

* Exactly what kind of life you will have if you don't change those things.
* 如果你不改变这些事情，你的生活会变成什么样

Then, sit with it for a week.
然后，花一周时间思考它。

Contemplate it.
沉思它。

Go on a walk and let your mind dance with those ideas.
去散步，让你的思绪与这些想法共舞。

After some time, and if you don't hide from the pain, you will start the next chapter of your life.
过一段时间，如果你不逃避痛苦，你就会开始人生的下一章。

## 自律不是建立的，是发现的 / Discipline Isn't Built, It's Discovered

I've been training in the gym for about 12 years now.
我已经在健身房训练大约12年了。

I've been writing every day for about 5.
我每天写作大约5年了。

I've walked 10k steps every day for about 4.
我每天走1万步大约4年了。

For all of the above, the first time I tried to form the habit, I fell off multiple times.
对于以上所有事情，我第一次尝试养成习惯时，失败了很多次。

I remember being invited on a walk and thinking it was for senile old people.
我记得被邀请去散步时，认为那是给老年痴呆的老人做的。

I was confirming the thoughts that helped me stay the same.
我在确认那些帮助我保持不变的 thoughts。

I didn't want to do those things but felt like I had to.
我不想做这些事情，但觉得必须做。

When I finally formed the habit, it was different than the other times I tried to force it.
当我最终养成习惯时，这与强迫自己时的情况不同。

They weren't "hard" to do.
做这些事情并不"难"。

They were the byproduct of experimentation and discovery.
它们是实验和发现的副产品。

## 习惯养成的三个原因 / Three Reasons Habits Formed

All of those habits became a part of my life for 3 specific reasons:
所有这些习惯成为我生活的一部分，有三个具体原因：

* I was deeply aware of a painful problem in my life
* 我深刻意识到生活中一个痛苦的问题

* I searched for evidence to support a desirable future version of myself
* 我寻找证据来支持一个理想的未来版本的自己

* I changed my physical and digital environments to solidify my new identity
* 我改变了我的物理和数字环境来巩固我的新身份

* I blocked out time during the week to do those things as if I were going to be doing them for the rest of my life
* 我在每周留出时间做这些事情，仿佛我要做一辈子

## 我的故事 / My Story

In middle school, my self-confidence was abysmal.
中学时，我的自信心极差。

I was extremely tall and lanky.
我非常高且瘦弱。

I felt awkward in my own skin, so I wanted to change that.
我觉得在自己的身体里很不自在，所以我想改变。

I used that pain as motivation to educate myself on fitness and nutrition.
我用那种痛苦作为动力来学习健身和营养。

I even remember reading "Nutrient Timing For Peak Performance" on my bus rides to school.
我甚至记得在坐校车去学校的路上读《巅峰表现的营养时机》。

I started doing push-ups in my room, started going to after-school weights, and my love for the gym picked up from there.
我在房间里做俯卧撑，开始去课后健身房，从此爱上了健身。

## 环境的力量 / The Power of Environment

To change my environment, every day after I got home from school, I would go straight to the computer and watch 2-3 of my favorite fitness vloggers on YouTube.
为了改变我的环境，每天放学回家后，我会直接打开电脑，在YouTube上看2-3个我最喜欢的健身博主。

They'd give me programs, exercises, and diet tips to try.
他们会给我提供训练计划、练习和饮食建议让我尝试。

They were my role models as a teenager, and they shaped most of what I do today.
他们是我青少年时期的榜样，塑造了我今天的大部分行为。

Don't neglect the positive power of social media.
不要忽视社交媒体的积极力量。

## 发现与实验 / Discovery and Experimentation

I didn't pick a random diet or training routine while forcing myself to stick with it because someone told me it was good to do.
我没有随意选择一个饮食或训练计划，然后强迫自己坚持，因为有人告诉我这样做是好的。

The common theme with people who are dissatisfied with their life is that they allow the confident advice of others to shape the majority of it.
对生活不满意的人的共同主题是，他们允许别人的自信建议来塑造他们的生活。

I treated this as a period of discovery and experimentation.
我把这段时期当作发现和实验的时期。

I was collecting vision.
我在收集愿景。

I was test-driving pieces of my future self.
我在试驾未来自己的各个部分。

## 读书的方法 / How I Read Books

I read the nutrition book because it sounded interesting.
我读那本营养书因为它听起来很有趣。

I couldn't help but be curious.
我忍不住好奇。

It could solve my problem.
它能解决我的问题。

As I read it, I wanted to try certain novel tactics I was learning, like eating 50g of simple carbs and 25g of protein before exercise like some Olympic athletes do.
当我读它时，我想尝试我学到的一些新策略，比如像一些奥运运动员一样在运动前吃50克简单碳水化合物和25克蛋白质。

This is how I read most books by the way.
这是我读大多数书的方式。

I don't force myself to read anything, and I don't read consistently.
我不强迫自己读任何东西，也不持续阅读。

I read books that I can't help but read, and I'm okay with going long periods without reading a thing.
我读那些我忍不住要读的书，长时间不读书也没关系。

## 实验的重要性 / The Importance of Experimentation

As I watched fitness vlogs, I found new exercises to try, and I was always excited to try them.
当我看健身视频时，我找到新的练习来尝试，我总是很兴奋地去尝试。

This wasn't difficult.
这并不困难。

It didn't require force.
不需要强迫。

It was fun to try things that could change my life.
尝试可能改变我生活的事情很有趣。

With time, all of the little things I learned morphed into a deep understanding of fitness and nutrition.
随着时间的推移，我学到的所有小事变成了对健身和营养的深刻理解。

I became my own teacher and trainer.
我成为了自己的老师和教练。

## 问题的根源 / The Problem

The problem here is that:
问题是：

(1) most people aren't hyper aware of a painful problem in their life, so their mind doesn't release dopamine at the sight of a potential solution
(1) 大多数人没有高度意识到生活中的痛苦问题，所以他们的大脑在看到潜在解决方案时不会释放多巴胺

(2) they learn without experimentation, so they never make the progress necessary to act as a feedback loop that keeps them motivated
(2) 他们学习而不实验，所以他们永远不会取得必要的进步来作为保持动力的反馈循环

## 如何工程化你的身份 / How to Engineer an Identity

### 第一步：认知 / Step One: Recognition

Who you are determines what you consider important.
你是谁决定了什么对你重要。

You must first recognize that you are pursuing a series of goals whether you are conscious of them or not.
你必须首先认识到，无论你是否意识到，你正在追求一系列目标。

Those goals frame how you interpret reality, and you usually notice things that confirm your current way of life.
这些目标框定了你如何解释现实，你通常会注意到那些证实你当前生活方式的事情。

The goals your mind operates on stem from conditioning, and conditioning stems from your environment.
你的大脑运作的目标源于条件作用，条件作用源于你的环境。

Your parents, teachers, and culture have put you on a path that is heading toward a dead end.
你的父母、老师和文化把你放在了一条走向死胡同的路上。

---

### 第二步：战略性不协调 / Step Two: Strategic Dissonance

To avoid getting trapped in a state of feeling lost – limbo – you let the divide between who you are and who you want to be grow wider.
为了避免陷入迷茫的状态——limbo——你让你是谁和你想成为谁之间的差距变得更大。

You cultivate dissatisfaction with your current lifestyle by noticing where you will end up if you don't change.
通过注意如果你不改变你会变成什么样，来培养对当前生活方式的的不满。

By becoming aware of the problems in your life, you prime your mind for pattern recognition.
通过对生活中问题的意识，你为大脑的模式识别做好了准备。

Your brain starts to confirm that change is the right thing to do and helps you notice the information that can aid in that.
你的大脑开始确认改变是正确的做法，并帮助你注意到有助于这一点的信息。

---

### 第三步：环境工程 / Step Three: Environment Engineering

Unfollow people who don't put out information that helps you achieve a better life.
取关那些不能帮助你过上更好生活的人。

Throw away the food that you allow yourself to eat at 3am.
扔掉你在凌晨3点允许自己吃的食物。

Pull out a paper and map out exactly what you are going to do that week down to the hour.
拿一张纸，精确规划你本周每小时要做什么。

Review and iterate every week.
每周回顾和迭代。

You need that feedback loop.
你需要那个反馈循环。

---

## 沉浸式学习 / Immersion

Immerse yourself in the people, places, and things that provide you with things to try.
沉浸在那些提供给你尝试事物的人、地方和事情中。

Follow new accounts even if you disagree with them.
即使你不同意也要关注新账号。

Scroll through Amazon until you find a book that sounds interesting, even if you only end up reading the first few pages.
在亚马逊上浏览直到找到一本听起来有趣的书，即使你最终只读了前几页。

Intention is what matters.
意图才是重要的。

Intention is you giving a direction for your mind to rewire itself toward.
意图是你给你的大脑一个自我重新连接的方向。

---

### 第四步：自我实验 / Step Four: Self-Experimentation

Because problems that aren't solved through experimentation aren't solved for good.
因为通过实验没有解决的问题不会永久解决。

Rather than being assigned another goal, you try everything until you find the one thing that you can't pull yourself away from.
而不是被分配另一个目标，你尝试一切直到找到那个让你无法放手的事情。

Then, you repeat that process until it becomes who you are.
然后，你重复这个过程直到它成为你是谁。

## 核心要点 / Key Takeaways

1. **你已经自律了** - 你只是对错误的目标自律
2. **痛苦是信号** - 不是要更努力，而是要改变方向
3. **身份决定行为** - 不是强迫自己做，而是成为那样的人
4. **实验中发现** - 不是坚持习惯，而是找到热爱
5. **环境塑造身份** - 沉浸在对的环境中最重要的是改变你的环境，让自己沉浸在正确的人群、内容和体验中。
