# 工程师正在成为巫师 | OpenAI Sherwin Wu 谈软件开发的未来

# "Engineers are becoming sorcerers" | The future of software development with OpenAI's Sherwin Wu

---

这是一档播客节目，邀请了 Sherwin Wu，他负责 OpenAI API 平台的工程管理工作。该平台约 95% 的工程师使用 Codex，他们通常同时运行 10 到 20 个并行 AI 代理。

This is a podcast episode featuring Sherwin Wu, who leads engineering for OpenAI's API platform, where roughly 95% of engineers use Codex, often working with fleets of 10 to 20 parallel AI agents.

### 讨论话题：

### Topics discussed:

1. OpenAI 如何将代码审查时间从 10-15 分钟缩短到 2-3 分钟
2. AI 如何改变管理者的角色
3. 为什么 AI 高效用户与其他人的生产力差距正在扩大
4. 为什么"模型会吃掉你的脚手架"
5. 为什么未来 12 到 24 个月是工程师在角色完全转型之前实现跃升的罕见窗口期

1. What OpenAI did to cut code review times from 10-15 minutes to 2-3 minutes
2. How AI is changing the role of managers
3. Why the productivity gap between AI power users and everyone else is widening
4. Why "models will eat your scaffolding for breakfast"
5. Why the next 12 to 24 months are a rare window where engineers can leap ahead before the role fully transforms
