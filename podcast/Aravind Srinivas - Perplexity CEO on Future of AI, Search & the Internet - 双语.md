## Key Quotes / 关键引言

### Perplexity Philosophy / Perplexity 哲学

**Aravind Srinivas:** "We never tried to play Google at their own game. If you're just trying to take on Google by building another search engine with some other differentiation, which could be privacy, or no ads, or something like that, it's not enough."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "我们从未试图在 Google 自己的领域与之竞争。如果你试图通过构建另一个具有某种差异化的搜索引擎来对抗 Google，无论是隐私、无广告还是其他什么，那是不够的。"

---

**Aravind Srinivas:** "The disruption comes from rethinking the whole UI itself. Why do we need links to be occupying the prominent real estate of the search engine UI? Flip that."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "颠覆来自于重新思考整个 UI 本身。为什么链接需要占据搜索引擎 UI 的突出位置？颠覆它。"

---

### Google Business Model / Google 商业模式

**Aravind Srinivas:** "It's the greatest business model in the last 50 years."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "这是过去 50 年最伟大的商业模式。"

---

### Larry Page & Sergey Brin / Larry Page 和 Sergey Brin

**Aravind Srinivas:** "They didn't compete with the other search engines by doing the same thing. They flipped it. They said, 'Hey, everyone's just focusing on text-based similarity... What if we instead ignore the text? We use the text at a basic level, but we actually look at the link structure and try to extract ranking signal from that instead.'"

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "他们没有通过做同样的事情与其他搜索引擎竞争。他们颠覆了它。他们说，‘嘿，每个人都只关注基于文本的相似性……如果我们忽略文本呢？我们在基本层面上使用文本，但实际上我们看链接结构，并试图从中提取排名信号。’"

---

**Aravind Srinivas:** "One philosophy I take from Larry is the user is never wrong. It's a very powerful profound thing. It's very simple but profound if you truly believe in it."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "我从 Larry 那里学到的一个哲学是用户永远不会错。这是一件非常强大而深刻的事情非常简单，但如果你真的相信它，它是深刻的。"

---

### Jeff Bezos / Jeff Bezos

**Aravind Srinivas:** "'Your margin is my opportunity.' Whose quote is that, by the way? Jeff Bezos."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "‘你的利润率是我的机会。’顺便问一下，这是谁的名言？Jeff Bezos。"

### Elon Musk / Elon Musk

**Aravind Srinivas:** "Elon, I've taken inspiration a lot for the raw grit. When everyone says it's just so hard to do something and this guy just ignores them and just still does it, I think that's extremely hard. It basically requires doing things through sheer force of will and nothing else."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "Elon，我从他的原始韧性中获得了很大灵感。当每个人都说什么都太难做的时候，这个人只是忽略他们仍然去做，我认为这非常难。这基本上需要通过纯粹的意志力去做事，别无其他。"

---

### Jensen Huang / Jensen Huang

**Aravind Srinivas:** "I met him once and I asked him, 'How do you handle the success and yet go and work hard?' And he just said, 'Because I am actually paranoid about going out of business. Every day I wake up in sweat thinking about how things are going to go wrong.'"

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "我见过他一次，我问他，‘你是如何在成功的同时仍然努力工作的？’他说，‘因为我实际上对破产感到偏执。每天我醒来都汗流浃背地想着事情会怎样出错。’"

---

### Yann LeCun / Yann LeCun

**Aravind Srinivas:** "He went through many years where people just ridiculed or didn't respect his work as much as they should have, and he still stuck with it. His positioning on unsupervised learning was super right and controversial at the time. That's literally the recipe for ChatGPT."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "他经历了多年人们只是嘲笑或不尊重他的工作，但他仍然坚持。他对无监督学习的立场在当时是非常正确和有争议的。这 literally 是 ChatGPT 的配方。"

---

### AI Breakthroughs / AI 突破

**Aravind Srinivas:** "Attention, parallel computation, transformer, scaling it up to do unsupervised pre-training, right data and then constant improvements."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "注意力、并行计算、transformer、扩展到无监督预训练、正确的数据，然后持续改进。"

---

**Aravind Srinivas:** "RLHF is the cherry on the cake. It's not easy to make these systems controllable and well-behaved without the RLHF step."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "RLHF 是蛋糕上的樱桃。没有 RLHF 步骤，这些系统不容易被控制和行为良好。"

---

### Product Design / 产品设计

**Aravind Srinivas:** "Products need to be having some magic to them and the magic comes from letting you be more lazy."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "产品需要有一些魔力，魔力来自于让你变得更懒。"

---

**Aravind Srinivas:** "The biggest enemy to us is not Google. It is the fact that people are not naturally good at asking questions. Curiosity is unbounded in this world. Every person in the world is curious, but not all of them are blessed to translate that curiosity into a well-articulated question."

> **Aravind Srinivas:** "我们最大的敌人不是 Google。而是人们天生不擅长提问这个事实。好奇心在这个世界上是无限的。世界上每个人都好奇，但不是所有人都有幸将好奇心转化为一个表达良好的问题。"

2. **Citation System**: Inspired by academic papers - every sentence needs a source
3. **Product Philosophy**: User is never wrong - build for lazy users
4. **Business Model**: Not trying to be as profitable as Google
5. **Founders' Wisdom**: Learn from Larry, Bezos, Elon, Jensen, Zuckerberg, Yann

7. **RLHF Role**: Important but not the core - cherry on the cake
8. **Future of Search**: AI-powered answer engines will coexist with traditional search
