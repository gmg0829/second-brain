## (00:00:00) – Orbital data centers

**Elon Musk:** The availability of energy is the issue. If you look at electrical output outside of China, everywhere outside of China, it's more or less flat. The output of chips is growing pretty much exponentially, but the output of electricity is flat. So how are you going to turn the chips on?

My prediction is that it will be by far the cheapest place to put AI. It will be space in 36 months or less. Maybe 30 months.

---

## (00:00:00) – 轨道数据中心

**Elon Musk:** 能源供应是关键问题。如果你看看中国以外的电力输出，中国以外各地基本上持平。芯片输出呈指数增长，但电力输出持平。那么你将如何启动芯片？

我的预测是，这将是目前最便宜的放置 AI 的地方。36 个月或更短时间内将是太空。也许 30 个月。

---

## (00:36:46) – Grok and alignment

**Elon Musk:** xAI's mission is to understand the universe. To understand the universe, you have to be curious and you have to exist. You can't understand the universe if you don't exist. So you want to increase the amount of intelligence in the universe.

Understanding the universe means you have to propagate intelligence into the future. It means you want to see how humanity develops. Any AI that is trying to understand the universe would want to see how humanity develops in the future, or else that AI is not adhering to its mission.

The central lesson for 2001: A Space Odyssey was that you should not make AI lie.

## (00:36:46) – Grok 和对齐

**Elon Musk:** xAI 的使命是理解宇宙。要理解宇宙，你必须好奇，你必须存在。如果不存在，你就无法理解宇宙。所以你想增加宇宙中的智能量。

理解宇宙意味着你必须将智能传播到未来。这意味着你想看到人类如何发展。任何试图理解宇宙的 AI 都会想知道人类未来如何发展，否则该 AI 就没有遵循其使命。

《2001太空漫游》的核心教训是：你不应该让 AI 说谎。

## (01:17:21) – Optimus and humanoid manufacturing

**Elon Musk:** Tesla is going to have a massive amount of AI edge compute. If you have AI edge compute, that's distributed power. The power is distributed over a large area. If you can charge at night, you can actually use the grid much more effectively.

For edge compute, Tesla is not constrained. We can make a lot of chips to make a very large number of robots and cars.

---

## (01:17:21) – Optimus 和类人机器人制造

**Elon Musk:** Tesla 将拥有大量 AI 边缘计算。如果你有 AI 边缘计算，那是分布式电力。电力分布在大面积上。如果你在夜间充电，你实际上可以更有效地利用电网。

对于边缘计算，Tesla 不受限制。我们可以制造大量芯片来制造大量机器人和汽车。

---

## (02:38:28) – TeraFab

**Elon Musk:** I've mentioned publicly the idea of doing a TeraFab, Tera being the new Giga. The limiting factor is chips. The limiting factor once you can get to space is chips, but the limiting factor before you can get to space is power.

To reach a large volume in say 36 months, to match the rocket payload to orbit... We need at least 100 gigawatts per year of solar. We need an equivalent amount of chips. My biggest concern is memory. The path to creating logic chips is more obvious than the path to having sufficient memory.

---

## (02:38:28) – TeraFab

**Elon Musk:** 我公开提到过做 TeraFab 的想法，Tera 是新的 Giga。限制因素是芯片。一旦你能到达太空，限制因素是芯片，但在你能到达太空之前，限制因素是电力。

要在 36 个月内达到大量产量，以匹配火箭有效载荷进入轨道……我们每年需要至少 100 吉瓦的太阳能。我们需要等量的芯片。我最大的担忧是内存。创建逻辑芯片的路径比获得足够内存的路径更明显。
