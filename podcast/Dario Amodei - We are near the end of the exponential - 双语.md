## (00:00:00) – What exactly are we scaling?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** We talked three years ago. In your view, what has been the biggest update over the last three years?

**Dario Amodei:** Broadly speaking, the exponential of the underlying technology has gone about as I expected it to go. There's plus or minus a year or two here and there. What has been the most surprising thing is the lack of public recognition of how close we are to the end of the exponential. To me, it is absolutely wild that you have people — within the bubble and outside the bubble — talking about the same tired, old hot-button political issues, when we are near the end of the exponential.

---

## (00:00:00) – 我们到底在扩展什么？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 我们三年前聊过。在你看来，过去三年最大的更新是什么？

**Dario Amodei:**  broadly speaking, 从根本上说，底层技术的指数增长大致如我所预期的那样进行。有时会有一两年的偏差。过去最令人惊讶的事情是，公众缺乏对我们离指数增长尾声有多近的认识。对我来说，这绝对令人震惊——无论是圈内还是圈外的人——在我们即将到达指数增长尾声的时候，人们还在讨论那些老旧的、令人厌倦的政治热点问题。

---

## (00:12:36) – Is diffusion cope?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** I want to understand what you mean by "the end of the exponential." Why do you think it's one year away and not ten years away?

**Dario Amodei:** There are two claims you could make here, one stronger and one weaker. On the basic hypothesis that within ten years we'll get to what I call a "country of geniuses in a data center," I'm at 90% on that. It's hard to go much higher than 90% because the world is so unpredictable. I maintain that there is something that's better to build, and I think everyone will want that. It's the AI that's robustly aligned to care about sentient life specifically.

---

## (00:12:36) – 扩散是借口吗？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 我想理解你所说的"指数增长尾声"是什么意思。你为什么认为是一年而不是十年？

**Dario Amodei:** 这里可以做出两个声明，一个更强，一个更弱。在我称之为"数据中心的天才之国"的基本假设下，我在10年内达到90%。因为世界如此不可预测，很难超过90%。我坚信有更好的东西需要构建，我认为每个人都会想要。那就是稳健对齐的AI，特别关心有感知能力的生命。

---

## (00:29:42) – Is continual learning necessary?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** When should we expect an AI system that can learn on the job like a human employee?

**Dario Amodei:** My guess for that is there's a lot of problems where basically we can do this when we have the "country of geniuses in a data center." My picture for that, if you made me guess, is one to two years, maybe one to three years. It's really hard to tell. I have a strong view—99%, 95%—that all this will happen in 10 years. I think that's just a super safe bet.

---

## (00:29:42) – 持续学习是必要的吗？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 我们什么时候可以期待一个像人类员工一样在工作中学习的AI系统？

**Dario Amodei:** 我的猜测是，当我们拥有"数据中心的天才之国"时，基本上很多问题都可以解决。如果让我猜，我想是一到两年，也许是一到三年。真的很难说。我有强烈的看法——99%，95%——这一切都会在10年内发生。我认为这是一个超级安全的赌注。

---

## (00:46:20) – If AGI is imminent, why not buy more compute?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** If you really believe we're going to have a country of geniuses, you want as big a data center as you can get. Why the conservatism?

**Dario Amodei:** It actually all fits together. We go back to this fast, but not infinitely fast, diffusion. Let's say that we're making progress at this rate. The technology is making progress this fast. I have very high conviction that we're going to get there within a few years. So there’s a little uncertainty on the technical side, but pretty strong confidence that it won’t be off by much.

---

## (00:46:20) – 如果AGI即将到来，为什么不多买计算资源？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 如果你真的相信我们会有一个天才之国，你会想要尽可能大的数据中心。为什么这么保守？

**Dario Amodei:** 实际上这一切都契合在一起。我们回到这个快速但不是无限快的扩散。假设我们正在以这个速度取得进展。技术正在以这样的速度快速进步。我有非常高的信念，我们将在几年内到达那里。所以在技术方面有一点不确定性，但有相当强的信心不会偏差太多。

---

## (00:58:49) – How will AI labs actually make profit?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** You've told investors that you plan to be profitable starting in 2028. Wouldn't this be exactly the time where you'd want to reinvest in the business?

**Dario Amodei:** Profitability is this kind of weird thing in this field. I don't think in this field profitability is actually a measure of spending down versus investing in the business. Let’s say half of your compute is for training and half of your compute is for inference. The inference has some gross margin that's more than 50%.

---

## (00:58:49) – AI实验室实际上将如何盈利？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 你告诉投资者你计划从2028年开始盈利。这不正是你想要再投资业务的时候吗？

**Dario Amodei:** 盈利性在这个领域是一种奇怪的东西。我不认为在这个领域，盈利性实际上是衡量支出与投资的指标。假设你一半的计算资源用于训练，一半用于推理。推理有一些超过50%的毛利率。

---

## (01:31:19) – Will regulations destroy the boons of AGI?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** It seems like the patchwork of state laws could prohibit many benefits that normal people could experience from AI.

**Dario Amodei:** I think that particular law is dumb. It was clearly made by legislators who just probably had little idea what AI models could do and not do. But I'm worried about it. Some people criticize my essay for saying, "That's too slow. The dangers of AI will come too soon if we do that." I think we need to be nimble.

---

## (01:31:19) – 监管会破坏AGI的好处吗？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 看起来各州法律的拼图可能会禁止普通人从AI中获得的许多好处。

**Dario Amodei:** 我认为那个特定的法律是愚蠢的。显然是由几乎不知道AI模型能做什么不能做什么的立法者制定的。但我担心这个。有些人批评我的文章说"这太慢了。如果这样做，AI的危险会很快到来。"我认为我们需要灵活应对。

---

## (01:47:41) – Why can't China and America both have a country of geniuses?

**Dwarkesh Patel:** Why shouldn't the US and China both have a "country of geniuses in a data center"?

**Dario Amodei:** If we have an offense-dominant situation, we could have a situation like nuclear weapons, but more dangerous. Either side could easily destroy everything. The other concern I have is that governments will oppress their own people with AI. My worry here is about governments. I'm worried about a world where you have a country in which there's already a government that's building a high-tech authoritarian state.

---

## (01:47:41) – 为什么中国和美国不能同时拥有天才之国？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 为什么美国和中国不应该同时拥有"数据中心的天才之国"？

**Dario Amodei:** 如果我们有一个进攻占主导的情况，我们可以有一个类似核武器的情况，但更危险。任何一方都可以轻易地摧毁一切。我担心的另一个问题是政府会用AI来压迫自己的人民。我担心的是政府。我担心的是一个已经有政府正在建设高科技威权国家的世界。
