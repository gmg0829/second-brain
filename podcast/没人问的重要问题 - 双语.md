## The Core Argument / 核心论点

### Government vs Anthropic

**Dwarkesh Patel:** The Department of War has declared Anthropic a supply chain risk because Anthropic refused to remove redlines around the use of their models for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons. 

The government's case was reasonable: "We don't ever want there to be a world where we become dependent on a private company for our warfighting, and then just have them cut us off if we cross their usage terms."

But instead of saying "We're not going to do business with you," the government threatened to destroy Anthropic as a private business.

---

### 政府 vs Anthropic

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 战争部宣布 Anthropic 为供应链风险，因为 Anthropic 拒绝取消将模型用于大规模监控和自主武器的红线。

政府的理由是合理的："我们永远不想看到一个世界，我们在战争方面依赖一家私人公司，然后如果我方违反使用条款，他们就直接切断我们的合作。"

但是，政府没有说"我们不和你做生意"，而是威胁要摧毁 Anthropic 这家私人企业。

---

### AI Structurally Favors Mass Surveillance

**Dwarkesh Patel:** There are 100 million CCTV cameras in America. You can get pretty good open source multimodal models for 10 cents per million input tokens. 

The yearly cost to process every single camera in America would be about $30 billion. And remember that AI ability gets 10x cheaper year over year—so by 2030, it might be cheaper for the government to understand what is going on in every single nook and cranny of this country than it is to remodel the White House.

Once the technical capacity for mass surveillance and political suppression exists, the only thing standing between us and an authoritarian surveillance state is the political expectation that this is not something we do here.

---

### AI 结构上利于大规模监控

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 美国有 1 亿个闭路电视摄像头。你可以通过开源多模态模型获得很好的处理，每百万输入 token 只需 10 美分。

处理美国每个摄像头的年度成本约为 300 亿美元。请记住，AI 能力每年便宜 10 倍——所以到 2030 年，政府可能更容易了解这个国家每个角落的情况，而不是重新装修白宫。

一旦大规模监控和政治压制的技术能力存在，我们与威权监控国家之间唯一剩下的就是政治期望，即这不是我们在这里做的事情。

---

### The Alignment Question / 对齐问题

**Dwarkesh Patel:** If alignment succeeded—that is, we figured out how to get AI systems to follow someone's intentions—then we would have "an army of extremely obedient employees."

The question is: **Alignment to whom?**

This is the most important question nobody's asking about AI. If AI is aligned to a private company, but the government has power over that company... then effectively the AI is aligned to the government.

---

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 如果对齐成功了——也就是说，我们弄清楚如何让 AI 系统遵循某人的意图——那么我们就会拥有一支"极度顺从的员工队伍"。

问题是：**对齐于谁？**

这是关于 AI 的最重要但没有人问的问题。如果 AI 对齐于一家私人公司，但政府对该公司有权力……那么实际上 AI 是对齐于政府的。

---

### What Does "America Winning" Mean? / "美国赢"意味着什么？

**Dwarkesh Patel:** What is the reason we want America to win the AI race? It's because we want to make sure free open societies can defend themselves.

We don't want the winner of the AI race to be a government which operates on the principle that there is no such thing as a truly private company or a private citizen. And that if the state wants you to provide them with a service on terms you find morally objectionable, you are not allowed to refuse.

Are we racing to beat the CCP in AI just so that we can adopt the most ghoulish parts of their system?

---

**Dwarkesh Patel:** 我们为什么想让美国赢得 AI 竞赛？是因为我们想确保自由开放的社会能够保卫自己。

我们不希望 AI 竞赛的赢家是一个以以下原则运作的政府：不存在真正的私人公司或私人公民这样的东西。如果国家希望你以你觉得在道德上令人反感的条款为他们提供服务，你不允许拒绝。

我们是为了在 AI 上击败 CCP 才竞赛的吗，这样才能采用他们系统中最可怕的部分？
