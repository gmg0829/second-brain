## (00:00:00) – Fairwater 2

**Satya Nadella:** We've tried to 10x the training capacity every 18 to 24 months. This would effectively be a 10x increase from what GPT-5 was trained with. The network optics in this building is almost as much as all of Azure across all our data centers two and a half years ago.

---

## (00:00:00) – Fairwater 2

**Satya Nadella:** 我们每 18-24 个月尝试将训练能力提高 10 倍。这实际上将比 GPT-5 训练所用的增加 10 倍。这栋建筑的网络光缆几乎相当于两年半前所有 Azure 数据中心的总和。

---

## (00:03:20) – Business models for AGI

**Satya Nadella:** I start with the excitement that maybe after the Industrial Revolution this is the biggest thing. But at the same time, I'm a little grounded in the fact that this is still early innings. We can either view AI as a "guardian angel" or a "cognitive amplifier." I love that metaphor. It's a simple way to think about what this is.

---

## (00:03:20) – AGI 商业模式

**Satya Nadella:** 我一开始很兴奋，也许继工业革命之后这是最大的事情。但与此同时，我有点扎根于这仍是早期阶段的事实。我们可以将 AI 视为"守护天使"或"认知放大器"。我喜欢这个比喻。这是一个简单的思考方式。

---

## (00:12:48) – Copilot competition

**Satya Nadella:** One thing I love about this chart is that we're still on top. Second, all these companies listed here are all companies born in the last four or five years. That's the best sign. You have new competitors, new existential problems.

The fact that we went from nothing to this scale is the market expansion. This is like the cloud-like stuff. Fundamentally, this category of coding and AI is probably going to be one of the biggest categories. It is the software factory category.

---

## (00:12:48) – Copilot 竞争

**Satya Nadella:** 关于这张图表，我最喜欢的一件事是我们仍然位居榜首。其次，这里列出的所有公司都是过去四五年成立的公司。这是最好的迹象。你有新的竞争对手，新的生存问题。

事实上，我们从无到有达到这个规模就是市场扩张。这就像云一样的东西。从根本上说，编码和 AI 这个类别可能是最大的类别之一。它是软件工厂类别。

---

## (00:20:02) – Whose margins will expand most?

**Satya Nadella:** Does all the value migrate just to the model? Or does it get split between the scaffolding and the model? I think time will tell. But my fundamental point is that the incentive structure gets clear.

If you win the scaffolding—which today is dealing with all the hobbling problems or the jaggedness of these intelligence problems—then you will vertically integrate yourself into the model.

---

## (00:20:02) – 谁的利润率扩张最多？

**Satya Nadella:** 所有价值都只是迁移到模型上？还是在脚手架和模型之间分配？我认为时间会证明一切。但我的根本观点是激励机制变得清晰。

如果你赢了脚手架——今天处理所有这些智能问题的束缚或参差不齐——那么你将垂直整合到模型中。

---

## (00:36:17) – MAI

**Satya Nadella:** At Microsoft, the way I look at it is that we are going to be in the hyperscale business, which will support multiple models. We will have access to OpenAI models for seven more years, which we will innovate on top of. We'll build our own models with MAI. We will always have a model level. And then we'll build—whether it's in security, knowledge work, coding, or in science—our own application scaffolding.

---

## (00:36:17) – MAI

**Satya Nadella:** 在 Microsoft，我看待这个问题的方式是，我们将进入支持多种模型的超大规模业务。我们将在未来 7 年继续使用 OpenAI 模型，并在此基础上进行创新。我们将用 MAI 构建我们自己的模型。我们将始终保持模型层面。然后我们将构建我们自己的应用脚手架——无论是在安全、知识工作、编码还是科学领域。
