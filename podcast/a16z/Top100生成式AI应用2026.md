# Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps: March 2026
# Top 100 生成式 AI 消费者应用：2026 年 3 月

*The latest results from our biannual deep-dive into AI apps*
*我们半年一次的 AI 应用深度调查最新结果*

---

## Introduction | 引言

Three years ago, we published the first edition of this list with a simple goal: identify which generative AI products were actually getting used by mainstream consumers. At the time, the distinction between "AI-first" companies and everything else was clear. ChatGPT, Midjourney, and Character.AI were purpose-built around foundation models. The rest of the software world was still figuring out what to do with the technology.

三年前，我们发布了这份榜单的第一版，目的很简单：找出哪些生成式 AI 产品实际上被主流消费者使用。当时，"AI 优先"公司和其他公司之间的区别很明显。ChatGPT、Midjourney 和 Character.AI 都是围绕基础模型专门构建的。软件世界的其他公司还在摸索如何利用这项技术。

That distinction no longer holds. CapCut, a video editor with 736 million monthly active mobile users, relies on AI for its most popular features - background removal, AI effects, auto-captions, and text-to-video generation. Canva has built its entire growth engine around its Magic Suite of AI tools. Notion has seen its paid AI attach rate surge from 20% to over 50% in a single year - AI features now account for roughly half of the company's ARR.

这种区别已不再成立。CapCut 是一款拥有 7.36 亿月活跃移动用户的视频编辑器，其最受欢迎的功能都依赖 AI——背景去除、AI 效果、自动字幕和文生视频。Canva 的整个增长引擎都围绕其 Magic Suite AI 工具构建。Notion 的付费 AI 附加率在一年内从 20% 飙升至超过 50%——AI 功能现在约占该公司 ARR 的一半。

From this edition onward, we're broadening the aperture to include any consumer product where generative AI has become a core part of the experience - including CapCut, Canva, Notion, Picsart, Freepik, and Grammarly. The result is what we believe is a more accurate picture of how people actually use AI, though the bulk of the top products continue to be AI-native.

从本版开始，我们扩大了范围，将生成式 AI 成为核心体验一部分的任何消费者产品纳入其中——包括 CapCut、Canva、Notion、Picsart、Freepik 和 Grammarly。我们认为，结果更能准确反映人们实际使用 AI 的方式，尽管排名靠前的产品大部分仍然是 AI 原生应用。

As always, our web ranking is based on unique monthly visits, per SimilarWeb as of January 2026. Mobile apps are ranked by monthly active users, per Sensor Tower as of January 2026.

和往常一样，我们的网页排名基于每月独立访问量，数据来源为 SimilarWeb，截至 2026 年 1 月。移动应用按月活跃用户排名，数据来源为 Sensor Tower，截至 2026 年 1 月。

## Key Takeaways | 核心要点

### 1. ChatGPT leads but the race for the "default AI" is on
### 1. ChatGPT 领先但"默认 AI"的竞争正在进行

ChatGPT is still far and away the largest consumer AI product. On web, it is 2.7x larger than the #2, Gemini (measured by monthly traffic) - and on mobile, it is 2.5x larger (measured by monthly active users). ChatGPT has seen weekly active users grow by 500 million people over the past year to 900 million today. This is especially impressive given growth is difficult to maintain at scale - over 10% of the global population now utilizes ChatGPT every week.

ChatGPT 仍然是迄今为止最大的消费者 AI 产品。在网页端，它的规模是第二名 Gemini 的 2.7 倍（按月流量计算）——在移动端，它的规模是 2.5 倍（按月活跃用户计算）。过去一年，ChatGPT 的周活跃用户增加了 5 亿，达到目前的 9 亿。尤其令人印象深刻的是，在如此大规模的背景下维持增长很困难——现在全球超过 10% 的人每周都在使用 ChatGPT。

But we're starting to see the category widening, with other horizontal platforms picking up steam for specific use cases. Both Gemini and Claude have seen an acceleration in their U.S. paid subscriber growth over the past year (though still dwarfed by ChatGPT - which is 8x larger than Claude and 4x larger than Gemini on this metric). Per Yipit Data, as of January 2026, Claude was growing paid subscribers by over 200% YoY - while Gemini was growing 258%.

但我们开始看到品类在扩大，其他横向平台在特定用例方面也在增长。过去一年，Gemini 和 Claude 在美国付费订阅用户增长方面都有加速（尽管与 ChatGPT 相比仍然相形见绌——在这一指标上，ChatGPT 的规模是 Claude 的 8 倍，是 Gemini 的 4 倍）。根据 Yipit Data 的数据，截至 2026 年 1 月，Claude 的付费订阅用户同比增长超过 200%——而 Gemini 增长 258%。

And, we're seeing increasing multi-tenanting behavior - with roughly 20% of weekly ChatGPT web users also using Gemini in a given week.

而且，我们看到越来越多的多平台使用行为——约 20% 的每周 ChatGPT 网页用户在同一周内也使用 Gemini。

**What happened? The competitors shipped.**

**发生了什么？竞争对手们推出了产品。**

Google crushed its creative models - Nano Banana generated 200 million images and brought 10 million new users to Gemini in its first week, and Veo 3 was widely regarded as the breakthrough moment for AI video. Anthropic doubled down on the prosumer with Cowork, Claude in Chrome, plug-ins to Excel and Powerpoint, and - most notably - Claude Code.

Google 在创意模型方面表现出色——Nano Banana 生成了 2 亿张图片，并在第一周为 Gemini 带来了 1000 万新用户，Veo 3 被广泛认为是 AI 视频的突破性时刻。Anthropic 加大力度投入专业消费者市场，推出了 Cowork、Chrome 版 Claude、Excel 和 Powerpoint 插件，以及最引人注目的 Claude Code。

The race matters not just for who leads today but for who becomes structurally difficult to displace. Context compounds: the more an LLM knows about you, the better results it can provide and the more you use it.

这场竞争不仅关乎谁目前领先，还关乎谁变得难以被取代。上下文会累积：一个 LLM 越了解你，它能提供的结果就越好，你使用它的频率就越高。

Early data suggests that sessions per user per month are climbing for Gemini on web, though they're still 1.3x higher on ChatGPT - and on mobile, ChatGPT dominates with 2.2x higher sessions per user per month. Both companies show best-in-class consumer paid subscriber retention in the U.S., per Yipit Data.

早期数据表明，Gemini 网页端每用户每月会话数在上升，尽管 ChatGPT 仍然高出 1.3 倍——在移动端，ChatGPT 以每用户每月高出 2.2 倍的会话数占据主导地位。根据 Yipit Data，两家公司都展现出美国市场最佳的消费者付费订阅留存率。

The next layer of lock-in is the app store. ChatGPT and Claude have both launched connector ecosystems - GPTs and Apps for ChatGPT, MCP integrations and Connectors for Claude - that let users build workflows on top of the assistant. Once a user has configured their AI to talk to their calendar, email, and CRM, switching costs rise dramatically.

下一层锁定是应用商店。ChatGPT 和 Claude 都推出了连接器生态系统——ChatGPT 的 GPTs 和应用、Claude 的 MCP 集成和连接器——让用户可以在助手之上构建工作流程。一旦用户配置好 AI 与其日历、邮件和 CRM 对话，切换成本就会大幅上升。

Developers may concentrate their efforts on the platforms that attract the most users, creating the same kind of flywheel that defined earlier platform wars.

开发者可能会将精力集中在吸引最多用户的平台上，创造出与早期平台战争相同的飞轮效应。

We're already starting to see where each platform is heading. Sam Altman has previously said OpenAI wants to "bring AI to billions of people who can't pay for subscriptions," which is why they've launched ads - and he's stated that OpenAI will launch a "Sign in with ChatGPT" identity layer, positioning the assistant as the default interface between consumers and the internet. The ambition is to make ChatGPT the starting point for everything: shopping, booking, browsing, health, and daily life.

我们已经可以看到每个平台的发展方向。Sam Altman 曾表示，OpenAI 想要"让数十亿无力支付订阅费用的人用上 AI"，这就是他们推出广告的原因——他还表示，OpenAI 将推出"用 ChatGPT 登录"的身份层，将助手定位为消费者与互联网之间的默认界面。目标是让 ChatGPT 成为一切的起点：购物、预约、浏览、健康和日常生活。

The app directories already reflect this. As of late February, ChatGPT's includes 220 apps across 13 categories. Claude has ~160 curated connectors plus ~50 community-built MCP servers. But the two share just 41 apps in common - roughly 11% of the combined catalog - and those 41 are almost entirely the horizontal productivity stack everyone needs: Slack, Notion, Figma, Gmail, Google Calendar, HubSpot, Stripe.

应用目录已经反映了这一点。截至 2 月底，ChatGPT 的目录包含 220 个应用，涵盖 13 个类别。Claude 有约 160 个精选连接器 plus 约 50 个社区构建的 MCP 服务器。但两者只有 41 个共同应用——约占合并目录的 11%——这 41 个应用几乎都是每个人都需要的横向生产力工具：Slack、Notion、Figma、Gmail、Google Calendar、HubSpot、Stripe。

Beyond that core, the platforms diverge almost completely. ChatGPT has 85+ apps across Travel, Shopping, Food, Health & Wellness, Lifestyle, and Entertainment - categories where Claude has virtually zero. These are consumer transaction categories: booking flights on Expedia, ordering groceries through Instacart, browsing listings on Zillow, tracking nutrition on MyFitnessPal. It's the most aggressive play any AI company has made to become a consumer super-app.

除核心之外，两个平台几乎完全分化。ChatGPT 在旅行、购物、食品、健康与健身、生活方式和娱乐方面有 85+ 个应用——而 Claude 在这些类别几乎为零。这些是消费者交易类别：在 Expedia 订机票、通过 Instacart 订购杂货、浏览 Zillow 列表、在 MyFitnessPal 上追踪营养。这是任何 AI 公司成为消费者超级应用的最激进举措。

Claude's exclusive integrations skew toward the professional: financial data terminals (PitchBook, FactSet, Moody's, MSCI), developer infrastructure (Sentry, Supabase, Snowflake, Databricks), science and medical tools (PubMed, Clinical Trials, Benchling), and a growing open-source MCP community that has no ChatGPT equivalent.

Claude 的独家集成偏向专业领域：金融数据终端（PitchBook、FactSet、Moody's、MSCI）、开发者基础设施（Sentry、Supabase、Snowflake、Databricks）、科学和医学工具（PubMed、临床试验、Benchling），以及一个不断成长的开源 MCP 社区，没有 ChatGPT 的对等物。

Anthropic appears to be focused on the AI power users (developers, knowledge workers, etc.) These users are more willing and able to pay for higher cost, direct subscriptions now.

Anthropic 似乎专注于 AI 高级用户（开发者、知识工作者等）。这些用户现在更愿意也有能力支付更高成本的直接订阅。

While ChatGPT has products catered to the same audience (ex. Codex, Frontier), they have also indicated a desire to be the platform for the true mainstream user - which may open up more avenues for monetization as the base continues to grow. They are already testing ads, and a take rate on transactions would also be a natural expansion.

虽然 ChatGPT 也有面向同一受众的产品（例如 Codex、Frontier），但他们也表明希望成为真正主流用户的平台——这可能会随着用户基础的持续增长而开辟更多盈利途径。他们已经在测试广告，交易抽成也将是自然的扩展。

If the AI assistant becomes not just a chat window but an operating environment, this race may end up looking less like the search wars - where one player took 90% of the market - and more like the mobile OS wars, where two platforms with very different philosophies both built trillion-dollar ecosystems.

如果 AI 助手不仅仅是一个聊天窗口，而是一个操作系统环境，这场竞争最终可能看起来不像搜索战争——当时一个玩家占据了 90% 的市场——而更像移动操作系统战争，两个拥有截然不同哲学观的平台都建立了万亿美元生态系统。

### 2. Global usage is splintering by product
### 2. 全球使用正在按产品分化

Geographically, the AI market is splintering into three distinct ecosystems, and the gaps between them are widening.

从地理上看，AI 市场正在分化为三个不同的生态系统，它们之间的差距正在扩大。

The Western AI tools continue to share a remarkably similar user base. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity all draw their top markets from the same pool: the US, India, Brazil, the UK, and Indonesia, in varying order. None has meaningful usage in China or Russia. This is a function of policy - Western tech sanctions have restricted access to US AI tools in Russia since 2022, and China requires AI providers to register, host data onshore, and comply with censorship rules.

西方 AI 工具继续共享非常相似的用户基础。ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 和 Perplexity 的主要市场都来自同一个池子：美国、印度、巴西、英国和印度尼西亚，顺序各有不同。在中国或俄罗斯都没有显著的使用量。这是因为政策——自 2022 年以来，西方科技制裁限制了俄罗斯对美国 AI 工具的访问，而中国要求 AI 提供商注册、在本地托管数据并遵守审查规则。

DeepSeek is the only product that bridges the divide. Its traffic splits across China (33.5%), Russia (7.1%), and the US (6.6%) on web, with a similar pattern on mobile. China is also a heavy user of Bytedance's Doubao and homegrown Kimi. Russia, which barely registered as a distinct market in our earlier editions, has emerged as a third pole with the second highest rate of Deepseek saturation.

DeepSeek 是唯一弥合这一分歧的产品。它的流量分布在网页端的中国（33.5%）、俄罗斯（7.1%）和美国（6.6%），移动端模式类似。中国也是字节跳动 Doubao 和国产 Kimi 的重度用户。俄罗斯——在我们前几版中几乎没有作为一个独立市场出现——已经成为第三个极点，DeepSeek 渗透率第二高。

Yandex Browser, which integrates the Alice AI assistant, reached 71 million MAU - making it a top ten mobile AI product globally. Sber's GigaChat debuted on our web list. The pattern mirrors what happened in China, but compressed: sanctions created the gap, and local products filled it within two years.

集成了 Alice AI 助手的 Yandex Browser 达到了 7100 万月活跃用户——成为全球移动 AI 产品前十名。Sber 的 GigaChat 首次出现在我们的网页榜单上。这种模式复制了中国发生的事情，但时间压缩了：制裁造成了差距，本土产品在两年内填补了空白。

To look at AI adoption on a per capita basis, we built a simple index combining web visits per capita and mobile MAU per capita across the products on our lists, scored from 0 to 100. The results reframe the geographic picture. Singapore ranks first, followed by the UAE, Hong Kong, and South Korea. The United States - the country that produced most of these products - ranks 20th.

为了按人均查看 AI 采用率，我们建立了一个简单指数，结合了我们榜单上产品的每千人网页访问量和每千人移动月活跃用户数，满分 100 分。结果重塑了地理图景。新加坡排名第一，其次是阿联酋、香港和韩国。美国——这些产品的大多数产地——排名第 20 位。

### 3. Creative tools make moves
### 3. 创意工具在移动

Midjourney, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion were the products that introduced most early adopters to generative AI - all three were released pre-ChatGPT. Image generation tools not only dominated creative tools (video and audio generation came later), but reigned supreme in our first three list versions of this list. The category has since evolved significantly.

Midjourney、DALL-E 和 Stable Diffusion 是向大多数早期采用者介绍生成式 AI 的产品——这三个产品都在 ChatGPT 之前发布。图像生成工具不仅主导了创意工具（视频和音频生成后来才出现），而且在我们前三版榜单中占据统治地位。该品类自此发生了显著演变。

In our first edition in September 2023, seven of the nine creative tools on the web list were image generators. Three years later, only three image generators remain - but seven creative tools still make the list. The difference is what fills the gap: video, music, and voice products have taken the slots that image generation vacated.

在我们 2023 年 9 月的第一版中，网页榜单上的九个创意工具中有七个是图像生成器。三年后，只有三个图像生成器仍然在榜上——但仍有七个创意工具上榜。填补空缺的是：视频、音乐和语音产品占据了图像生成腾出的位置。

The image story is one of bundling. As the native image models within ChatGPT (GPT Image 1.5) and Gemini (nano banana) improved, the bar for standalone image products rose sharply. On our very first edition of this list, Midjourney ranked in the top 10 - it's since fallen to #46. The products that remain - Leonardo, Ideogram, CivitAI - tend to serve specific creative communities with opinionated features rather than competing on general-purpose generation.

图像的故事是捆绑。随着 ChatGPT（GPT Image 1.5）和 Gemini（nano banana）内置的图像模型改进，独立图像产品的门槛急剧上升。在我们第一版榜单中，Midjourney 排名前十——现在已经跌至第 46 位。剩下的产品——Leonardo、Ideogram、CivitAI——倾向于服务特定创意社区，具有鲜明特色，而不是在通用生成方面竞争。

Video generation saw the most movement this edition. Kling AI, Hailuo, and Pixverse have all built real traction, with Chinese-developed models consistently leading in output quality - we would not be surprised to see Seedance 2.0-based applications on the next list. Veo 3 was the first US model to close that gap, and has driven traffic to Google Labs (which rose from #36 to #25 in the ranks).

视频生成在这一版中变化最大。Kling AI、Hailuo 和 Pixverse 都建立了真正的吸引力，中国开发的模型持续在输出质量上领先——我们不会惊讶于在下版榜单中看到基于 Seedance 2.0 的应用。Veo 3 是第一款缩小差距的美国模型，并推动了 Google Labs 的流量（排名从第 36 位升至第 25 位）。

What's missing? Sora. OpenAI launched the 2.0 version of its flagship video model as a standalone app in September 2025 - users were able to upload their digital likeness as a Cameo, and generate videos featuring real people. Sora spent 20 days at the top of the U.S. App Store and reached 1 million downloads faster than ChatGPT. Since then, downloads have decreased, as Sora stopped virally exploding as a social app (no one has yet cracked AI x social) - preventing it from making the mobile list for this edition. But, SensorTower still counts upwards of 3 million daily active Sora users on mobile, as AI video creators continue to leverage the model even if they cross-publish outputs elsewhere.

缺少的是什么？Sora。OpenAI 于 2025 年 9 月推出了其旗舰视频模型的 2.0 版本作为独立应用——用户能够上传他们的数字形象作为 Cameo，并生成包含真人的视频。Sora 在美国 App Store 榜首待了 20 天，比 ChatGPT 更快达到 100 万次下载。自那时起，下载量有所下降，因为 Sora 不再作为社交应用病毒式爆发（还没有人破解 AI x 社交）——这使其无法进入本版移动榜单。但 SensorTower 仍然统计到移动端超过 300 万的 Sora 日活跃用户，因为 AI 视频创作者继续利用该模型，即使他们在其他地方交叉发布输出。

Music and voice have been more defensible. Suno (#15) retained its rank from the last version of the list. ElevenLabs has appeared on every edition since September 2023; its capabilities - voice cloning, dubbing, audio production - remain specialized enough that they haven't been replicated as a checkbox feature.

音乐和语音更具防御性。Suno（第 15 名）保持在上版榜单的排名。ElevenLabs 自 2023 年 9 月以来每版都出现；其能力——语音克隆、配音、音频制作——仍然足够专业化，还没有作为一个勾选功能被复制。

The pattern: where the model giants and incumbents like Google and OpenAI have focused their creative efforts (image, increasingly video), standalone traffic compresses - though there is still room for more opinionated and perhaps more highly monetized products that serve users beyond the mainstream. Where they haven't (music, voice), there's more room.

这种模式：在模型巨头和现有企业如 Google 和 OpenAI 专注于创意努力的领域（图像，越来越多的视频），独立流量会压缩——尽管仍有空间容纳更多具有鲜明特色、可能更高变现的产品来服务主流以外的用户。在他们没有关注的领域（音乐、语音），有更多空间。

### 4. Agents are here
### 4. Agent 已经到来

The shift toward agentic AI didn't start this edition - it started last edition, with vibe coding. When Lovable, Cursor, and Bolt appeared on our March 2025 list, they represented something new: AI products that didn't just answer questions or generate media, but built things on the user's behalf. That was agentic behavior, scoped to a single vertical.

向 Agent AI 的转变不是从这一版开始的——它从上一版开始，即氛围编程。当 Lovable、Cursor 和 Bolt 出现在我们 2025 年 3 月的榜单时，它们代表了一些新事物：AI 产品不只是回答问题或生成媒体，而是代表用户构建东西。那是 Agent 行为，局限在单个垂直领域。

Vibe coding has proved retentive among a technical (and semi-technical) audience - with Replit and Lovable represented on this edition, as well as Claude Code (via Claude). There's more growth to come, as the trend has not yet cracked the true mainstream. Traffic across the top five vibe coding platforms has continued, though slowed from the initial explosion - though revenue continues to rise for many of these products as developers and teams use them more intensely.

氛围编程已被证明对技术（和半技术）受众具有留存性——Replit 和 Lovable 在这一版出现，Claude Code（通过 Claude）也是。随着这一趋势尚未突破真正的主流，还有更多增长空间。前五大气氛编程平台的流量持续增长，尽管从最初的爆发放缓——尽管许多产品的收入持续增长，因为开发者和团队更密集地使用它们。

More recently, horizontal agents have started to emerge. In January 2026, an open-source project called OpenClaw went from a solo developer's side project to 68,000 GitHub stars and mainstream coverage in a matter of weeks. Built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw is a locally run AI agent that connects to your messaging apps and executes multi-step tasks on your behalf. If ChatGPT was the moment consumers discovered AI could talk, OpenClaw may be the moment they discovered AI could act.

最近，横向 Agent 开始出现。2026 年 1 月，一个名为 OpenClaw 的开源项目在几周内从独立开发者的副项目变成了 6.8 万 GitHub 星标和主流报道。由奥地利开发者 Peter Steinberger 构建，OpenClaw 是一个本地运行的 AI Agent，连接到你的消息应用并代表你执行多步骤任务。如果 ChatGPT 是消费者发现 AI 可以说话的时刻，OpenClaw 可能是他们发现 AI 可以行动的時刻。

The product took off in the developer community - if our analysis was pushed to February (not January), OpenClaw would have ranked in the top 30 of our web list. And yet, OpenClaw is not (yet!) consumer-grade - it requires knowledge of Terminal to set up and maintain.

该产品在开发者社区起飞——如果我们的分析推迟到 2 月（而不是 1 月），OpenClaw 本应排在我们网页榜单的前 30 名。然而，OpenClaw 还不是消费级产品——它需要 Terminal 知识来设置和维护。

OpenClaw has continued to pick up traction among technical users, and in early March became the most-starred project on Github - surpassing both React and Linux. However, the product has not yet "graduated" to the true mainstream user - at least, as measured by new visits to the OpenClaw setup web domain (which have been fairly flat). The product was acquired by OpenAI in February 2026, which perhaps indicates the possibility of an even more accessible version of OpenClaw coming soon.

OpenClaw 继续在技术用户中获得吸引力，3 月初成为 Github 上最受星标的项目——超越 React 和 Linux。然而，该产品还没有"毕业"到真正的主流用户——至少，根据新访问 OpenClaw 设置网页域的访问量衡量（相当平稳）。该产品于 2026 年 2 月被 OpenAI 收购，这可能表明更易访问的 OpenClaw 版本可能即将推出。

OpenClaw isn't the only horizontal agent on the list. Both Manus and Genspark made the ranks - each platform allows consumers to hand over open-ended tasks (research, spreadsheet analysis, slide generation), and AI will handle the workflow end-to-end. This is Manus's second time on the list, and since it debuted it was acquired by Meta in December 2025 for an estimated $2 billion. Genspark debuts on this edition - the company raised a $300M Series B earlier this year, and announced a $100M revenue run rate.

OpenClaw 不是榜单上唯一的横向 Agent。Manus 和 Genspark 都上榜了——每个平台都允许消费者交出开放性任务（研究、电子表格分析、幻灯片生成），AI 将端到端处理工作流程。这是 Manus 第二次上榜，自其亮相以来于 2025 年 12 月被 Meta 以约 20 亿美元收购。Genspark 在这一版首次亮相——该公司今年初完成了 3 亿美元的 B 轮融资，并宣布了 1 亿美元的经常性收入率。

On mobile, consumers generally interact with agents via text - not via mobile apps. At setup, users connect OpenClaw to platforms like WhatsApp, Telegram, and Signal; users message it like they'd message a friend, and it executes tasks in the background. Other products like Poke similarly provide an agentic experience directly via SMS.

在移动端，消费者通常通过文本与 Agent 互动——而不是通过移动应用。在设置时，用户将 OpenClaw 连接到 WhatsApp、Telegram 和 Signal 等平台；用户像发消息给朋友一样给它发消息，它在后台执行任务。Poke 等其他产品同样通过短信直接提供 Agent 体验。

These products will compete with the agentic capabilities of the general LLM assistants that consumers use every day - ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. As they build their own connective tissues with Connectors and apps, will consumers use one of these products as their primary agent? The next six months will give us a good picture.

这些产品将与消费者每天使用的通用 LLM 助手的 Agent 能力竞争——ChatGPT、Claude 和 Gemini。随着它们通过连接器和应用建立自己的连接组织，消费者会使用其中一种产品作为他们的主要 Agent 吗？未来六个月将给我们一个清晰的图景。

### 5. AI beyond the browser or app
### 5. 浏览器或应用之外的 AI

Every prior edition of this list has ranked AI products by two metrics: web visits and mobile MAU. But a new class of AI products is emerging that these rankings cannot capture. Some of the most significant consumer AI growth of the past year happened in products that don't register in either metric.

此前每版榜单都通过两个指标对 AI 产品进行排名：网页访问量和移动月活跃用户。但一类新的 AI 产品正在出现，榜单无法捕捉这些。去年一些最重要的消费者 AI 增长发生在无法用这两个指标衡量的产品中。

The most visible shift is the browser itself becoming an AI product. In the past nine months, OpenAI launched Atlas (a browser with ChatGPT built into every page), Perplexity shipped Comet, and the Browser Company (since acquired by Atlassian) launched Dia. Data from Yipit shows that Perplexity's Comet has made the biggest dent in the market (in terms of visits to the download page), though no AI browser has seen accelerating growth.

最明显的转变是浏览器本身正在成为 AI 产品。过去九个月，OpenAI 推出了 Atlas（一个在每个页面内置 ChatGPT 的浏览器），Perplexity 推出了 Comet，Browser Company（后来被 Atlassian 收购）推出了 Dia。来自 Yipit 的数据显示，Perplexity 的 Comet 在市场上留下了最大的印记（就下载页面访问量而言），尽管没有 AI 浏览器看到加速增长。

Other AI giants are choosing to add AI to an existing browser, versus launching an AI browser as a standalone product. Google added Gemini to Chrome and released Disco in beta-testing, which dynamically generates web apps based on a user's browser tab. Anthropic released Claude in Chrome, which can connect to a user's Claude or Claude Code session to drive action on the web.

其他 AI 巨头选择将 AI 添加到现有浏览器，而不是推出独立的 AI 浏览器。Google 将 Gemini 添加到 Chrome 并发布了 Disco 正在 beta 测试，它可以根据用户的浏览器标签动态生成网络应用。Anthropic 发布了 Chrome 版 Claude，可以连接到用户的 Claude 或 Claude Code 会话以在网络上驱动操作。

Desktop-native AI tools have seen even more dramatic growth, especially for developers. Claude Code - a command-line agent for developers - reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in just six months. OpenAI shipped a standalone Codex app for Mac, with the company reporting total Codex weekly active users of 2 million as of early March, growing 25% weekly. And, Cursor retained its spot on our top 50 web list.

桌面原生 AI 工具的增长更为显著，尤其是对开发者而言。Claude Code——一个面向开发者的命令行 Agent——在短短六个月内达到了 10 亿美元的年度经常性收入率。OpenAI 为 Mac 推出了独立的 Codex 应用，该公司报告称截至 3 月初 Codex 周活跃用户总数为 200 万，每周增长 25%。并且，Cursor 保持在我们前 50 网页榜单上的位置。

For pure consumers, the most common standalone AI desktop applications are voice-related. Notetakers like Fireflies, Fathom, Otter, TL;DV, and Granola, have reached users via PLG and increasingly saturated enterprises - with a combined 20M visitors combined across the top five players. Workspace apps like Notion (debuting on this list), have also increasingly integrated AI via notetakers, research agents, and even task automation.

对于纯消费者来说，最常见的独立 AI 桌面应用与语音相关。Fireflies、Fathom、Otter、TL;DV 和 Granola 等笔记应用通过 PLG 触达用户，并日益渗透企业——前五名玩家合计拥有 2000 万访问量。Notion（在这一版首次亮相）等工作区应用也越来越多地通过笔记应用、研究 Agent 甚至任务自动化集成 AI。

Finally, AI is increasingly embedded in the tools people already use. Anthropic launched Claude in Excel and Claude in PowerPoint. OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Excel. Google has deepened Gemini's integration across Workspace - Docs, Sheets, Gmail, and Meet all now have native AI capabilities. Google also launched Personal Intelligence in January 2026, connecting Gemini to Gmail, Google Photos, YouTube, and Search - so the assistant can reference your hotel booking, your purchase history, your photo library, and your watch habits without being told.

最后，AI 越来越嵌入到人们已经使用的工具中。Anthropic 推出了 Excel 版 Claude 和 PowerPoint 版 Claude。OpenAI 推出了 Excel 版 ChatGPT。Google 深化了 Gemini 在整个工作区的集成——Docs、Sheets、Gmail 和 Meet 现在都有原生 AI 能力。Google 还于 2026 年 1 月推出了个人智能，将 Gemini 连接到 Gmail、Google Photos、YouTube 和 Search——因此助手可以参考你的酒店预订、购买历史、照片库和观看习惯，无需你告诉它。

The implication for this list: our rankings increasingly undercount the AI products people use most. A developer who spends eight hours a day in Claude Code and a knowledge worker who dictates every email through Wispr are heavy AI users who barely register in web traffic data. We're measuring AI adoption with a flashlight when we need a floodlight.

对这个榜单的启示：我们的排名越来越低估人们最常使用的 AI 产品。一个每天在 Claude Code 中花费八小时的知识工作者，以及一个通过 Wispr 口述每封邮件的人，都是重度 AI 用户，但在网页流量数据中几乎看不到。我们在用灯笼测量 AI 采用率，但实际需要探照灯。

As AI moves from a destination to a feature, our methodology will need to shift.

随着 AI 从目的地转变为功能，我们的方法论需要改变。
