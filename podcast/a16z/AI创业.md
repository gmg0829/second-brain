# AI for Startups
# AI 创业政策

## 第一部分 / Part 1

Artificial intelligence is the most consequential innovation we have seen in a generation, with the transformative power to address society's most complex problems and create a whole new economy—much like what we saw with the advent of the printing press, electricity, and the internet.

人工智能是我们这一代最具影响力的创新，具有解决社会最复杂问题和创造全新经济的变革力量——就像我们过去看到印刷术、电力和互联网的出现一样。

---

It is a moment so big that it would be shortsighted for us to ignore the power of collaborating across the AI ecosystem—and the possibilities and potential that stand to be born from it.

这一刻如此重大，如果我们忽视 AI 生态系统合作的力量以及可能从中诞生的可能性和潜力，那就太目光短浅了。

---

That is what brings us together—a shared understanding of what the stakes of this moment require and the role innovators big and small play in expanding opportunity and maintaining economic competitiveness.

这就是将我们团结在一起的原因——我们共同认识到这一时刻的风险要求，以及大小创新者在扩大机会和维护经济竞争力方面发挥的作用。

---

Our two firms believe that Little Tech and Big Tech can work successfully together, both to build a broader innovation ecosystem and collaborate on public policy initiatives.

我们两家公司相信，小型科技和大型科技可以成功合作，既可以建设更广泛的创新生态系统，也可以在公共政策举措上开展合作。

---

## 第二部分 / Part 2

Our two companies might not agree on everything, but this is not about our differences. It is about jointly recognizing that the policy choices—or missteps—we make now will determine whether the U.S. can continue our long and proud history of fostering innovation and seeing startups, small businesses, and entrepreneurs succeed.

我们两家公司可能在一切问题上意见不一致，但这不是关于我们的分歧。这是关于共同认识到我们现在做出的政策选择——或失误——将决定美国是否能继续我们长期以来引以为豪的创新传统，并让初创企业、小企业和企业家取得成功。

---

After all, we both know a thing or two about the little guy working to achieve greatness from their garage. That is the story of Microsoft and the mission of a16z.

毕竟，我们都知道一些关于车库创业实现伟大成就的故事。这就是微软的故事，也是 a16z 的使命。

---

## 第三部分 / Part 3

We both firmly believe that when it comes to AI, the opportunities are enormous and that our way forward to building a new AI economy is by spurring innovation and fostering competition.

我们都坚定地相信，在 AI 领域，机会是巨大的，我们建设新 AI 经济的方式是刺激创新和促进竞争。

---

The best way to encourage innovation while also ensuring safety and security is through a variety of responsible market-based approaches and business models, including open-source AI.

在鼓励创新同时确保安全和安全的最佳方式，是通过各种负责任的市场化方法和商业模式，包括开源 AI。

---

Open-source models have contributed indispensably to major advances in technology and research for decades, often by reducing or eliminating power imbalances between major institutions and scrappy upstarts, allowing the academic and startup community to have greater access and build upon existing knowledge while also offering real-time peer review.

几十年来，开源模式为技术和研究的重大进步做出了不可或缺的贡献，通常是通过减少或消除主要机构与充满活力的新贵之间的权力不平衡，让学术界和初创企业社区能够获得更多机会并在现有知识基础上进行建设，同时提供实时同行评审。

---

## 第四部分 / Part 4

a16z views competition and innovation through the lens of Little Tech: small businesses and startups.

a16z 通过小型科技的视角来看待竞争和创新：小企业 和初创企业。

---

a16z published its policy agenda for Little Tech in July, proposing a series of policy ideas to promote AI innovation through regulation that creates an even playing field across all industries.

a16z 在 7 月发布了小型科技政策议程，提出了一系列通过监管促进 AI 创新的政策想法，以在各行业创造公平竞争环境。

---

The goal of the Little Tech Agenda is to ensure that public policy supports startups, providing entrepreneurs the "freedom to research, to invent, to create jobs, to build the future."

小型科技议程的目标是确保公共政策支持初创企业，为企业家提供"研究的自由、发明的自由、创造就业的自由、构建未来的自由"。

## 第五部分 / Part 5

Microsoft is committed to making large-scale AI infrastructure investments that only a Big Tech company with our scope and size can afford, creating a platform that is affordable and easily accessible to everyone, including startups and small firms.

微软承诺进行只有我们这种规模和大型科技公司才能负担的大规模 AI 基础设施投资，创建一个所有人都能负担得起且易于访问的平台，包括初创企业和小公司。

---

These infrastructure investments are essential to creating opportunities for new businesses to experiment and grow in the AI economy.

这些基础设施投资对于为新企业在 AI 经济中实验和成长创造机会至关重要。

---

In February of this year, Microsoft announced a set of principles (referred to as the AI Access Principles) to govern its operations and deliver on this commitment.

今年 2 月，微软宣布了一套原则（称为 AI 访问原则）来管理其运营并履行这一承诺。

---

One primary tenet that informs Microsoft's AI Access Principles is the critical need to "spur innovation and competition across the new AI economy."

指导微软 AI 访问原则的一个主要信条是"在新 AI 经济中刺激创新和竞争"的关键需求。

Together, we believe in a competitive and broad ecosystem that harnesses the potential of academic research and business innovation, including a vital role for open-source innovation that will unleash the ideas of tomorrow to make life better for everyone and our country more secure and prosperous.

我们共同相信一个竞争激烈且广泛的生态系统，能够发挥学术研究和商业创新的潜力，包括开源创新的重要作用，这将释放明天的想法，让每个人的生活更美好，让我们的国家更安全、更繁荣。

By coming together, we hope to drive innovation and creativity and further live up to our highest aspirations and ideals that have defined the U.S. for generations.

通过团结起来，我们希望推动创新和创造力，进一步实现我们的最高愿望和理想，这些愿望和理想定义了几代美国人的特质。

## 政策建议 / Policy Recommendations

### 1. 促进美国企业机会的监管

U.S. AI laws and regulations should support the global success and proliferation of U.S. technology companies by promoting access and opportunity. This can be done by leveraging a science and standards-based approach that recognizes regulatory frameworks that focus on the application and misuse of technology. Regulation should be implemented only if its benefits outweigh its costs. In accounting for costs, policymakers should include an assessment of possible costs associated with unnecessary bureaucratic burdens to startups. As the new global competition in AI evolves, laws and regulations that mitigate AI harm should focus on the risk of bad actors misusing AI and aim to avoid creating new barriers to business formation, growth, and innovation.

美国的 AI 法律法规应该通过促进准入和机会来支持美国科技公司的全球成功和普及。这可以通过利用基于科学和标准的方法来实现，该方法认可专注于技术应用和误用的监管框架。只有当监管的收益超过成本时才应实施。在计算成本时，政策制定者应评估与初创企业不必要的官僚负担相关的可能成本。随着 AI 领域新全球竞争的演变，减轻 AI 伤害的法律法规应侧重于不良行为者滥用 AI 的风险，并旨在避免为企业形成、增长和创新创造新障碍。

---

### 2. 竞争与选择

Enabling choice and broad access fosters AI innovation and competition. Regulators should not only permit providers to offer a broad array of models—proprietary and open source, large and small—but should permit developers and startups the flexibility to choose which AI models to use wherever they are building solutions and not tilt the playing field to advantage any one platform. Developers should have the freedom to choose how to distribute and sell their AI models, tools, and applications for deployment to customers.

启用选择和广泛访问促进 AI 创新和竞争。监管机构不仅应允许提供商提供广泛的模型——专有和开源，大和小——还应允许开发者和初创企业灵活选择在构建解决方案时使用哪些 AI 模型，而不是将竞争环境偏向任何单一平台。开发者应该有自由选择如何分发和销售他们的 AI 模型、工具和应用程序，以部署给客户。

---

### 3. 开源创新

Open-source software provides immense value to our economy by catalyzing the innovation ecosystem. It allows tech companies big and small the ability to build the next innovation quickly and gives them a wide array of tools for developing software safely, securely, and competitively. We believe the same is true for open-source AI models. They increase choice and allow startups to more easily develop fine-tuned systems and applications. The free availability and performance of these models allow startups to access, use, and benefit from AI by modifying it to suit their conditions and diverse needs. They also offer the promise of safety and security benefits, since they can be more widely scrutinized for vulnerabilities. Regulators and decision-makers should embrace a regulatory framework that protects open source and secures the ability of entrepreneurs, startups, and companies to create, build, transform and win the future.

开源软件通过催化创新生态系统为我们的经济提供巨大价值。它让大小科技公司都能快速构建下一个创新，并为他们提供广泛的工具，以安全、可靠和有竞争力的方式开发软件。我们相信开源 AI 模型也是如此。它们增加了选择，并让初创企业更容易开发微调系统和应用程序。这些模型的免费可用性和性能让初创企业能够通过修改 AI 来适应其条件和多样化需求，从而访问、使用并从 AI 中受益。它们还提供安全和安保方面的好处承诺，因为它们可以更广泛地接受漏洞审查。监管机构和决策者应该采用一个保护开源并确保企业家、初创企业和公司能够创造、构建、转型和赢得未来的监管框架。

---

### 4. 开放数据共享

Data is a critical input for all AI developers. There is a role for government to enable and craft policies that support a thriving and growing ecosystem of data around the globe through Open Data Commons—pools of accessible data that would be managed in the public's interest. Governments should participate and lead this effort by releasing data sets in ways that are useful for AI cultural institutions and libraries. Governments should ensure that startups can easily access these data pools.

数据是所有 AI 开发者的关键输入。政府有责任通过开放数据共享——以公众利益管理的可访问数据池——来支持和制定支持全球数据繁荣发展的政策。政府应该参与并领导这一努力，以对 AI 文化机构和图书馆有用的方式发布数据集。政府应该确保初创企业能够轻松访问这些数据池。

---

### 5. 学习权

Copyright law is designed to promote the progress of science and useful arts by extending protections to publishers and authors to encourage them to bring new works and knowledge to the public, but not at the expense of the public's right to learn from these works. Copyright law should not be co-opted to imply that machines should be prevented from using data—the foundation of AI—to learn in the same way as people. Knowledge and unprotected facts, regardless of whether contained in protected subject matter, should remain free and accessible.

版权法旨在通过延长对出版商和作者的保护来促进科学和实用艺术的进步，鼓励他们向公众带来新作品和知识，但不是以牺牲公众从这些作品中学习的权利为代价。版权法不应该被劫持来暗示机器应该被阻止使用数据——AI 的基础——以与人类相同的方式学习。知识和不受保护的事实，无论是否包含在受保护的主题中，都应该保持自由和可访问。

---

### 6. 投资AI

The U.S. government should invest in AI to accelerate American innovation, strengthen our national security, and create economic opportunity. As part of this investment strategy, the government should examine its procurement practices to enable more startups to sell technology to the government.

美国政府应该投资 AI 以加速美国创新、加强我们的国家安全并创造经济机会。作为这一投资战略的一部分，政府应该审查其采购实践，以使更多初创企业能够向政府销售技术。

---

### 7. 帮助人们在 AI 时代繁荣

Building a new AI economy that supports startups, and American entrepreneurship will require public policy that cultivates technical talent and engages digital citizens. To that end, policy should fund digital literacy programs that help people understand how to use AI tools to create and access information. It should also support workforce skill development and workforce retraining programs to help people secure jobs in an AI-driven economy.

构建支持初创企业和美国创业的新 AI 经济需要培养技术人才和吸引数字公民的公共政策。为此，政策应该资助数字素养项目，帮助人们理解如何使用 AI 工具创建和访问信息。它还应该支持劳动力技能发展和劳动力再培训计划，以帮助人们在 AI 驱动的经济中获得就业。

---

## 结语 / Conclusion

We obviously live in a tumultuous time, often characterized by disagreements among people and groups. But as in every age, we need to seize opportunities to find common ground. The United States has built a tech ecosystem that has helped fuel the nation's economic growth for more than half a century. As reflected here, we believe that Little Tech and Big Tech can contribute even more in the decades ahead by coming together. We can build on each other's strengths, and we can advocate together for public policies that will serve innovation and the nation's broader interests.

我们显然生活在一个动荡的时代，人们和群体之间经常存在分歧。但就像每个时代一样，我们需要抓住机会寻找共同点。美国建立了一个科技生态系统，在半个多世纪以来帮助推动国家经济增长。正如这里所反映的，我们相信小型科技和大型科技可以通过团结在未来几十年做出更大贡献。我们可以相互借鉴优势，我们可以共同倡导服务于创新和国家更广泛利益的公共政策。
